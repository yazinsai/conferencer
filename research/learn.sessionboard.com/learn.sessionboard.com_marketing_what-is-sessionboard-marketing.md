# What is Sessionboard Marketing?

Marketing turns your event into content and demand. Sessions are captured as they happen, catalogued into a searchable library attributed to the speakers who delivered them, and distributed through the people with the most credible reach — your speakers, sponsors, and your own team.

It isn’t a separate distribution tool bolted on the side. It runs on the same speaker, session, and event data you already manage in Sessionboard, which is why a clip knows who said it and a share link knows which speaker it belongs to.

## How it fits together

[Section titled “How it fits together”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/what-is-sessionboard-marketing#how-it-fits-together)

The three phases of an event each feed the next.

**Before** — speakers submit bios and materials through their portal, and your AV team gets everything they need in the [Ready Room](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/ready-room) without needing accounts.

**During** — [Live Transcribe](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/live-transcribe) captures each session from the soundboard and produces a speaker-attributed transcript, an AI summary, and extracted themes within minutes of the session ending.

**After** — the [Media library](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/media-library) holds everything, searchable by topic, speaker, or keyword. [Create](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/create) drafts content from it, [Advocacy](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy) puts it in your speakers’ hands with tracked links, and last year’s content promotes next year’s event.

## Where content is attributed

[Section titled “Where content is attributed”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/what-is-sessionboard-marketing#where-content-is-attributed)

Every transcript, quote, clip, and share links back to the speaker who delivered it. Over time that builds an institutional record of who spoke on what, how it performed, and who your most effective advocates are — searchable across your whole event history rather than trapped in one event’s folder.

- Engagement tracking: who shared what and how it performed
- Subject-matter expert discovery by topic across your speaker network
- Cross-event content attribution per speaker

## Common situations

[Section titled “Common situations”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/what-is-sessionboard-marketing#common-situations)

**“Our event ended three weeks ago and we still haven’t published anything.”** Transcripts and summaries are ready minutes after each session. Clips and quotes come out of the library rather than an edit queue, and Advocacy campaigns launch in days.

**“On event day the AV team asks for speaker files ten minutes before each session.”** The Ready Room gives them assets, schedules, and transcription controls through a magic link — no accounts, no onboarding.

**“We run four events a year and the content ends up in a Dropbox folder nobody revisits.”** The Media library centralizes everything, searchable by topic and speaker, so past content promotes future events.

**“We want speakers to share but we never know whether they did.”** Advocacy gives each speaker a personal tracked link, so shares and clicks attribute to the individual rather than disappearing.

## Integrations

[Section titled “Integrations”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/what-is-sessionboard-marketing#integrations)

Marketing is the content layer, not a replacement for your marketing stack.

| Integration | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini | MCP / AI | Query Sessionboard data from your AI tool of choice, no manual exports |
| Goldcast, Zoom, and other webinar platforms | Video ingest | Import recordings and transcripts into the Media library |
| Cvent | Event management | Event data connected and continuously enriched |
| Gleanin & Snöball | Distribution | For organizations with existing contracts; Advocacy replaces these long term |

The MCP integration is read-only and token-scoped, with PII obfuscated by default and a 90-day audit log.
