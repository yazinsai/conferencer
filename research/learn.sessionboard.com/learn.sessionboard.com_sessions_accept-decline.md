# Accept or decline submissions

After a speaker submits a session, it appears in the **Sessions** module. Admins review the submission — along with any evaluator feedback from [Evaluation Plans](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/evaluation-plans) — to approve or deny it.

## The five statuses

[Section titled “The five statuses”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/accept-decline#the-five-statuses)

| Status | Meaning | What the speaker sees in their portal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ✅ **Accepted** | Officially approved and included in the event; typically shown on the agenda. | Accepted |
| 📥 **Accepted queue** | Recommended or held for acceptance, not yet finalized. | Pending |
| ⏳ **Pending** | Default status for newly submitted sessions; awaiting review. | Pending |
| 📥 **Decline queue** | Being considered for rejection, not yet declined. | Pending |
| ❌ **Declined** | Officially rejected and excluded from the event. | Declined |

Once a session is set to **Accepted**, it becomes visible in the [Agenda](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/agenda) for your event team.

## Review submissions

[Section titled “Review submissions”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/accept-decline#review-submissions)

### Review all submissions

[Section titled “Review all submissions”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/accept-decline#review-all-submissions)

Open the **Sessions** module and adjust your dashboard view to include the session questions from your submission form.

### Review submissions individually

[Section titled “Review submissions individually”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/accept-decline#review-submissions-individually)

In the **Sessions** module, select the pencil icon next to the session you want to review.

## Update a session’s status

[Section titled “Update a session’s status”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/accept-decline#update-a-sessions-status)

In the **Sessions** module, click the current status of a session, then choose the new status from the dropdown. You can also update statuses in bulk.
