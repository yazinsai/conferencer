# Create sponsor groups & contacts

When a sponsor completes the intake form, a new sponsor contact and group are created automatically. Admins can also add groups and contacts manually.

## Add a sponsor group

[Section titled “Add a sponsor group”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors#add-a-sponsor-group)

Add

In the **Sponsor** module, click the **Add** button in the top right.

Add new group

Select **Add New Group** in the pop-up.

Enter details

Add the sponsoring organization’s information (name, phone, address).

Save

Click **Save**, or **Save & Add People** to add contacts right away.

## Add a sponsor contact

[Section titled “Add a sponsor contact”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sponsors-exhibitors/sponsors#add-a-sponsor-contact)

Open the Primary Contact cell

In the group’s **Primary Contact** column, click **\+ Add Contact** (or the existing contact to add more).

Create a new contact

Click **\+ Create a new contact** in the pop-up.

Enter details

Add the contact’s information and set **Record Type** to **Sponsors**.

- Mark as **primary contact** to make them the recipient of admin communications.
- Mark to **give portal access** so they can log in via the portal link (no email is sent automatically).

Add

Click **Add** at the bottom of the pop-up.
