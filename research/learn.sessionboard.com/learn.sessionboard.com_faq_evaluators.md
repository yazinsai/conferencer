# Email your event team and evaluators

Emails go to users listed in your event’s Contacts module, so add someone there first if you need to reach them.

![Email your event team and evaluators](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/a4ca70ff-image-png-Feb-02-2026-07-34-14-9887-PM.png)

## Recommendation

[Section titled “Recommendation”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/evaluators#recommendation)

To send emails to your team or evaluators, we recommend the following:

1. Add your event team and/or evaluators to the Contacts module in your event.
2. Consider importing your contacts to save time!
3. Once added, you can send them a custom or targeted email using the standard email tools available within Sessionboard.

👉🏽Learn how to create & send emails.

> 💡 Note: Adding team members or evaluators as contacts does not affect their access permissions unless otherwise configured.
