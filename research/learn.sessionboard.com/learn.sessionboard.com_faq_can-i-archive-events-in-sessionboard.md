# Archive an event

In Sessionboard, there is no formal “archive” feature for events. All events you create — past, present, or future — will remain accessible in your account unless manually deleted by an administrator.

## Why Isn’t Archiving Available?

[Section titled “Why Isn’t Archiving Available?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/can-i-archive-events-in-sessionboard#why-isnt-archiving-available)

Our platform is designed to allow teams to retain full access to past event data for reference, reporting, and duplication purposes. This ensures you can easily:

- View past contacts and session information
- Clone previous event setups (e.g., tasks, forms, templates)
- Maintain a historical record for internal use or audit purposes
