# Save a submission as a draft

When submitting a session proposal, you may not always have all the required information on hand. The Save as Draft feature allows you to begin your submission and return to complete it later—without losing any progress.

* * *

## Save a draft submission

[Section titled “Save a draft submission”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/participants/save-a-submission-as-a-draft#save-a-draft-submission)

To save your submission as a draft ensure your proposed submission has at least a Title and select the button “Save as draft” in the bottom right-hand corner of the form.

![Save a draft submission](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e5920aaa-Screenshot-2025-04-21-at-1.29.32-PM.png)

Once your submission is saved as a draft, a banner will appear at the top of the submission highlighting that you are editing a draft submission.

![Save a draft submission](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/220ff575-Screenshot-2025-04-21-at-1.31.59-PM.png)

## Access a saved draft

[Section titled “Access a saved draft”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/participants/save-a-submission-as-a-draft#access-a-saved-draft)

When you access the submission form link and log back in, the system will prompt you to resume your draft submission.

![Access a saved draft](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/7297216c-Screenshot-2025-04-21-at-1.35.46-PM.png)

## Discard a Saved Draft

[Section titled “Discard a Saved Draft”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/participants/save-a-submission-as-a-draft#discard-a-saved-draft)

To discard your saved draft submission select “Reset saved data” to the right of your screen.

![Discard a Saved Draft](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/c9f4c47a-Screenshot-2025-04-21-at-1.33.30-PM.png)
