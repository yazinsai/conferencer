# Email templates

Creating email templates allows you to easily email your event contacts. The email template can include merge fields for session, contact, sponsor, or exhibitor-specific details.

## Create email templates

[Section titled “Create email templates”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/settings/email-templates#create-email-templates)

To create an email template, navigate to Settings > Email Templates within your event dashboard and click the “Add Template” button at the top right-hand corner of your screen.

There is no limit to how many templates you can create. Templates will be ordered alphabetically (A-Z) by the template name.

![Create Email Templates](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/a426ca11-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-27-38-3571-PM.png)

These templates can not be deleted.

![These templates can not be deleted](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/61242d98-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-28-05-2439-PM.png)

Within the pop-up window, customize your email to include:

❗IMPORTANT: If you change a template module after you begin typing the message body, the current merge tags will not be valid. Please ensure to reapply your merge tags as they differ per module.

![Within the pop-up window, customize your email to include](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/a907b9fe-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-28-37-6402-PM.png)

| Template Name: Internal Name Type: This determines the merge fields available to use as well as where the template can be used within the platform. Groups: Exhibitor and Sponsor Modules Contacts: Contacts, Speakers, Chairpersons, Moderators, and Session Submitters Modules Sessions: Sessions Module Reply To: The email address that should receive replies to the email sent. We recommend using a shared inbox or an event-specific email address. This field only supports one email address. Send From (only available if you have purchased the use of a custom email domain): Specify the email address you would like your email to be sent from.  CC: Send a copy of your email to up to five visible email addresses that you want to include on the thread.  BCC: Send a copy of your email to up to five hidden email addresses. Subject Line: The subject line of the email Message Body: Customize the body of the email with the use of merge tags |  |

To manage the actions for an email template, select the three dots to the right of the template name.
