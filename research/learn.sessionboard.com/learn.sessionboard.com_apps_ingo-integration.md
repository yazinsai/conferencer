## Step 1: Generate the Widget Script in InGo

[Section titled “Step 1: Generate the Widget Script in InGo”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/apps/ingo-integration#step-1-generate-the-widget-script-in-ingo)

1. Log into your InGo Dashboard.
2. Navigate to the ‘Install Widgets page’
3. Under “Select Registration Provider”, choose “Sessionboard”.
4. The Social Widget will populate.
5. Copy the URL from the Social Widget field in InGo.

## Step 4: Embed the Social Share Widget

[Section titled “Step 4: Embed the Social Share Widget”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/apps/ingo-integration#step-4-embed-the-social-share-widget)

1. Place your cursor where you want the widget to appear.
2. Click the three-dot helix icon on the right-hand side.
3. Select “Source Code”.

![Step 4: Embed the Social Share Widget](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/606b9f30-Screenshot-2026-07-08-at-3.15.39-PM.png)

4. You will then want to add your source code accordingly. Please be sure that you are including iframe tags in your source code.

![Step 4: Embed the Social Share Widget](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/85c5fed7-Screenshot-2026-07-08-at-3.17.49-PM.png)
An example would be as follows (the URL bolded would be replaced by your social share URL from your InGo campaign : <iframe src=“ [https://app.ingo.me/widgets/iframe?widget\_unique\_id=123456789ABCDEF](https://app.ingo.me/widgets/iframe?widget_unique_id=123456789ABCDEF)” width=“100%” height=“600” frameborder=“0”></iframe>
5\. Adjust the sizing, width, and height as needed.
6\. Click Save.

## Step 5: Add the Wiki Page to Your Portal

[Section titled “Step 5: Add the Wiki Page to Your Portal”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/apps/ingo-integration#step-5-add-the-wiki-page-to-your-portal)

Once the Wiki page is saved, you can now add it to any Speaker Portal or Sponsor/Exhibitor Portal of your choosing by going to Portals > Portals > Edit Tasks. In the screenshot below, you can name the Wiki Page “Share Your Participation”. The naming of this page is at your discretion and can be customized during the page creation to ensure it aligns with your event needs:
