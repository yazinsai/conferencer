# Email campaigns

## Accessing email campaigns

[Section titled “Accessing email campaigns”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#accessing-email-campaigns)

Email Campaigns is available at the event level. To access it, open your event, then navigate to Collect & Preview > Send from the left-hand menu.

![Accessing Email Campaigns](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/9188d48e-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-48-15-1365-PM.png)

The dashboard organizes campaigns by status:

- All: every campaign regardless of status
- Draft: campaigns in progress, not yet sent or scheduled
- Scheduled: campaigns queued for a future send
- Sending: campaigns currently in delivery
- Sent: completed campaigns
- Paused: campaigns that have been temporarily stopped
- Cancelled: campaigns that have been cancelled

Filter by Campaign name, Status, Type, Send date, or Creator.

From the Settings menu you can also reach:

- Email Templates: reusable email layouts
- Themes: visual branding for your emails
- History: a log of all email activity
- Analytics: performance metrics across campaigns
- Unsubscribe: contacts who have opted out

![Unsubscribe](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/0e16d9d3-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-50-54-5878-PM.png)

## Managing email templates

[Section titled “Managing email templates”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#managing-email-templates)

Templates define the content and structure of your campaigns. Navigate to Settings → Email Templates to manage them.

![Managing Email Templates](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/0b8f6351-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-52-18-1352-PM.png)

### Creating a template

[Section titled “Creating a template”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#creating-a-template)

Click Add Template and configure:

- Template Name: internal reference name
- Sender Name & Email: the “From” display name and address
- Reply-To Address: where replies will be directed
- CC Recipients: additional recipients to copy
- Subject Line: the email subject
- Email Body: the message content (supports rich text and merge tags)

### Reusing Templates Across Events

[Section titled “Reusing Templates Across Events”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#reusing-templates-across-events)

Use Copy From to import a template from another event in your organization — handy for recurring events.

## Creating a campaign

[Section titled “Creating a campaign”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#creating-a-campaign)

To create a new campaign, click Create Campaign from the Email Campaigns dashboard.

![Creating a Campaign](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e66f2a52-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-53-58-1799-PM.png)

Sessionboard guides you through a five-step workflow.

### Step 1: Select Your Audience Type

[Section titled “Step 1: Select Your Audience Type”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#step-1-select-your-audience-type)

Select the audience type:

![Step 1: Select Your Audience Type](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/cfcce61a-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-54-43-7598-PM.png)

IndividualsSend personalized emails to individual participants: Speakers, Moderators, Authors, Attendees, or any contact type.

CompaniesSend to Sponsors and Exhibitors, uniformly or customized per company.

SessionsTarget sessions and reach Speakers, Chairs, and Moderators assigned to them.

📌 Note: The available merge tags change depending on the audience type you select. If you switch audience types after composing your message, you will need to reapply your merge tags.

### Step 2: Compose Your Message

[Section titled “Step 2: Compose Your Message”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#step-2-compose-your-message)

Write your email content, including:

- Subject Line
- Email Body
- Sender Name & Address
- Reply-To Settings

![Step 2: Compose Your Message](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/8eb821b8-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-55-43-6257-PM.png)

Use merge tags to personalize content. See the Merge Tags Reference section below for the full list.

### Step 3: Select Recipients

[Section titled “Step 3: Select Recipients”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#step-3-select-recipients)

Define who receives the campaign. Sessionboard shows the eligible recipient count with advanced filter options.

![Step 3: Select Recipients](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/0b3e4431-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-56-34-9290-PM.png)

Filter recipients by:

- Participant Role — Author, Speaker, Moderator, Panelist, etc.
- Submission Status — target based on submission progress
- Acceptance Status — filter by participation confirmation
- Session Information — target participants by session assignment
- Custom Fields — use any additional contact data for precise targeting

You can also add recipients from outside the event database when needed.

### Step 4: Preview & Test

[Section titled “Step 4: Preview & Test”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#step-4-preview--test)

Review the campaign exactly as recipients will see it before sending.

![Step 4: Preview & Test](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/bc80cb8b-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-58-27-7797-PM.png)

From the preview screen you can:

- Verify layout, content, and branding
- Check merge tag rendering
- Send a test email to any address to validate the final result

### Step 5: Send or Schedule

[Section titled “Step 5: Send or Schedule”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#step-5-send-or-schedule)

Choose your delivery method:

![Step 5: Send or Schedule](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/7bc850f8-image-png-Jul-07-2026-06-59-16-9995-PM.png)

- Send Now: delivers the campaign immediately
- Schedule for Later: set a specific future date and time for delivery
- Recurring Campaign: set up an automated schedule that sends regularly without manual intervention (e.g. weekly reminders, follow-up sequences)

## Analytics & History

[Section titled “Analytics & History”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#analytics--history)

### Analytics

[Section titled “Analytics”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#analytics)

Analytics show campaign performance across your event.

![Analytics](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/52274e7c-image-png-Jul-07-2026-07-00-26-6870-PM.png)

Key metrics include:

- Open Rate: Percentage of recipients who opened the email
- Click Rate: Percentage who clicked at least one link
- Bounce Rate: Emails that could not be delivered
- Unsubscribe Rate: Recipients who opted out
- Total Sent: Number of emails delivered

For deeper analysis, use Sessionboard’s Insights: AI Reports & Custom Dashboards to build custom email reports.

![For deeper analysis, use Sessionboard’s Insights](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/3020f099-image-png-Jul-07-2026-07-00-47-6944-PM.png)

[Section titled “History”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#history)

History logs all sent campaigns and email activity for your event. Review past sends, delivery details, and earlier communications.

Unsubscribe Management

The Unsubscribe section shows contacts who opted out of email communications for your event. Monitor this to maintain healthy communication and evaluate campaign relevance.

Unsubscribed contacts will not receive future campaigns from this event, even if they match the recipient filters.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

### My merge tags are showing as placeholder text instead of real data, what happened?

[Section titled “My merge tags are showing as placeholder text instead of real data, what happened?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#my-merge-tags-are-showing-as-placeholder-text-instead-of-real-data-what-happened)

This usually means the audience type was changed after the message was composed. Go back to Step 2, remove the broken merge tags, and reinsert them using the merge tag selector for your current audience type.

### Why do I see fewer recipients than expected in Step 3?

[Section titled “Why do I see fewer recipients than expected in Step 3?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#why-do-i-see-fewer-recipients-than-expected-in-step-3)

The recipient count only includes contacts matching your active filters who have a valid email address. Check that filters aren’t too restrictive and that profiles include an email. Unsubscribed contacts are excluded automatically.

### Can I edit a scheduled campaign?

[Section titled “Can I edit a scheduled campaign?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#can-i-edit-a-scheduled-campaign)

Yes. Open the campaign from the dashboard and click Edit. Make your changes and re-save. Note that if the campaign is actively sending, editing is not available until delivery is complete.

### What’s the difference between a Recurring Campaign and scheduling for later?

[Section titled “What’s the difference between a Recurring Campaign and scheduling for later?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#whats-the-difference-between-a-recurring-campaign-and-scheduling-for-later)

Scheduling for later sends the campaign once at a specific time. A Recurring Campaign sends automatically on a repeating schedule (daily, weekly, etc.) without manual intervention — useful for ongoing communication sequences.

### Can I pause or cancel a campaign that’s already scheduled?

[Section titled “Can I pause or cancel a campaign that’s already scheduled?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#can-i-pause-or-cancel-a-campaign-thats-already-scheduled)

Yes. From the campaign dashboard, click the … menu on the campaign card and select Pause or Cancel.

### What happens if a recipient is on the unsubscribe list?

[Section titled “What happens if a recipient is on the unsubscribe list?”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/email-campaigns#what-happens-if-a-recipient-is-on-the-unsubscribe-list)

Unsubscribed contacts are automatically excluded from all campaigns, regardless of the filters applied in Step 3.
