# Create & manage evaluation plans

Evaluation Plans gather feedback on submitted sessions from evaluators using rating scales. Navigate to **Sessions → Evaluation → Evaluation Plans** and click **Add Plan**.

## Build a plan

[Section titled “Build a plan”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/evaluation-plans#build-a-plan)

Type

Select **Assign Evaluators**.

Configuration

- **Name** and **Instructions** (criteria and scoring guidance)
- **Set the plan as open** — enable to set a due date now, or open later
- **Enable anonymized review** — hides speaker names
- **Enable weekly reminders** — automated Monday emails to evaluators
- **Include uploaded files** — give evaluators access to session files

Evaluators

Assign Evaluators, Evaluator Session Managers, and Admin Users.

Session filters

Filter by standard and custom session fields to assign specific submissions.

Display fields

Choose which session and speaker fields evaluators see, and create custom evaluation questions (e.g., a “Would you recommend this session?” dropdown, external/internal comment boxes).

Grading options

- **Rating icon** — faces/numbers (1–5) or stars/hearts (1–20)
- **Enable rubric** — weighted criteria totaling 100% (max 255 characters per criterion)
- **Evaluation limits** — max evaluations per submission

## Manage & open plans

[Section titled “Manage & open plans”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/evaluation-plans#manage--open-plans)

- Manage reusable fields under **Content → Fields**.
- To open a closed plan, click the ellipsis and choose **Open and notify evaluators** or **Open and do not send notifications**.
- Other actions: **Review** (progress and ratings), **Edit**, **Export** (Individual or Cumulative Grades Report), **Delete** (permanent), **Duplicate**, and **Notify New Evaluators**.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/evaluation-plans#faq)

Do ratings automatically reach speakers?

No. Ratings and comments are only accessible to event admins with access to plan results.

Can evaluators see other evaluators' comments?

No — only admins can access evaluation results.

Can evaluators communicate with speakers?

No, evaluators have view-only access through their assigned plans.
