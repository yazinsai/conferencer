# AI evaluations

AI Evaluations automate the speaker submission review process through AI-generated scoring, summaries, and feedback, using customizable **virtual evaluator personas**.

- 🚀 Accelerate speaker selection with AI-generated feedback and scoring
- 💡 Improve consistency with customizable reviewer personas
- 🔒 Save hours of manual work without sacrificing content quality

## Improve results with complete event details

[Section titled “Improve results with complete event details”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/ai-evaluations#improve-results-with-complete-event-details)

To maximize AI effectiveness, complete these in **Settings → Event Details**: **Event Type**, **Event Website URL**, **Event Location**, and **Theme**. These help the AI understand your event’s nature, audience, and goals.

## Evaluator personas

[Section titled “Evaluator personas”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/ai-evaluations#evaluator-personas)

Personas are AI-powered virtual evaluators that review sessions from diverse perspectives — a technical expert, a first-time attendee, an executive decision-maker — giving you comparative feedback on quality, relevance, and impact. (Create them in [Program settings](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/program-settings).)

## Create an AI evaluation plan

[Section titled “Create an AI evaluation plan”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/ai-evaluations#create-an-ai-evaluation-plan)

Add a plan

In the **Evaluations** module, open **Evaluation Plans** and click **Add Plan**.

Type

Select **Virtual Evaluators**.

Configuration

Set a name, detailed instructions, and whether to include uploaded files.

Evaluators

Select the personas (custom or default) to use.

Session filters & display fields

Assign submissions and choose the fields personas can see.

Grading options

Choose a rating icon (faces/numbers 1–5 or stars/hearts 1–20) and optionally enable a weighted rubric (criteria total 100%, max 255 characters each).

Save & generate

Click **Save Plan**, then generate reviews immediately or later via **⋯ → Regenerate Evaluations**.

## View results

[Section titled “View results”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/ai-evaluations#view-results)

AI plans show a blue icon next to the plan name. Use the ellipsis to **Review** ratings and feedback, or **Export** an Individual or Cumulative Grades Report.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/ai-evaluations#faq)

How is my data protected?

Sessionboard uses a closed AI processing model over private, secured API channels with TLS 1.2+ encryption and strict access controls. Data is not retained for model training.

How do I make results more personalized?

Enrich the event theme, website URL, and plan instructions — write them as if onboarding a new team member.

Can I try again if I don't like the results?

Yes — regenerate evaluations, duplicate plans with new instructions, or create new plans. There’s no limit on plans, personas, or attempts.
