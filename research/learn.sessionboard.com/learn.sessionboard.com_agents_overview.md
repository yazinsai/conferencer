# Agents overview

Agents are supervised AI teammates that act on your event graph — sourcing speakers, drafting evaluations, packaging content, and more. They run at a tunable level of autonomy, with every action logged and reversible.

## What agents can do

[Section titled “What agents can do”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/agents/overview#what-agents-can-do)

Source speakers

Research and enrich prospects in the [CRM Pipeline](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/pipeline) — logged in the card’s Activity feed under **Agent**.

Evaluate submissions

Generate scores, summaries, and feedback via [AI Evaluations](https://learn.sessionboard.com/evaluations/ai-evaluations) personas.

Build the agenda

Draft and organize sessions with the [AI agenda builder](https://learn.sessionboard.com/studio/ai-agenda-builder).

Report & analyze

Answer questions in natural language through [Insights](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai) and SbQL.

## Autonomy & trust

[Section titled “Autonomy & trust”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/agents/overview#autonomy--trust)

Agents operate at supervised autonomy levels — they propose and act within scope, but you stay in control:

- **Activity logging** — every agent action appears in the relevant record’s Activity feed, distinct from **User**, **System**, and **Rule** events.
- **Scoped access** — agents inherit token scopes and PII settings; see [API tokens](https://learn.sessionboard.com/integrations/api-tokens).
- **Reversible** — agent changes are auditable in [History](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai) and can be rolled back.

## Connect your own AI assistant

[Section titled “Connect your own AI assistant”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/agents/overview#connect-your-own-ai-assistant)

Any MCP-compatible assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) can act as an agent against your event data via the Sessionboard MCP server. See [API tokens → Connect AI assistants](https://learn.sessionboard.com/integrations/api-tokens).
