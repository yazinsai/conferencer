# View & edit your submission

You can edit your submission any time **before the submission close date**.

Log into your portal

See [how to access your portal](https://learn.sessionboard.com/participants/access-portal).

Open your session

In the **My Sessions** widget, click the session you want to edit.

View submission

At the bottom of the sidebar, select **View Submission** to open and edit your form.
