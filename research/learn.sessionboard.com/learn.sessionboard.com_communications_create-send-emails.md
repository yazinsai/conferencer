# Create & send emails

Sessionboard lets you email event participants directly from the platform.

## Create an email

[Section titled “Create an email”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/create-send-emails#create-an-email)

From the **Contacts**, **Sessions**, **Sponsors**, or **Exhibitors** module, check the records you want to email. In **Contacts**, click **Send → Send Emails**; other modules have a direct **Send Emails** button.

## Configure the email

[Section titled “Configure the email”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/create-send-emails#configure-the-email)

In the pop-up, set the left-side fields:

- **Who should receive this email?** — the primary recipient(s), with an option to **include additional contacts**
- **Replies sent to** — where replies arrive (one address only)
- **Send from** — the sending address
- **CC / BCC** (optional) — up to five addresses each, comma-separated

Compose the subject and body on the right, and click **Template** to apply a pre-built template.

## Review & send

[Section titled “Review & send”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/create-send-emails#review--send)

Click each contact to preview how the email renders for that recipient, then click **Send Now**.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/create-send-emails#faq)

What's the default sender address?

All emails send from `no-reply@notify.sessionboard.com` unless your event uses a [custom email domain](https://learn.sessionboard.com/communications/create-send-emails).

Can I send a test first?

The Review & Preview page shows how each recipient will see the email before you send.

Can I attach files?

Upload files to the portal instead, where users can download them directly.

Do additional contacts get CC'd?

Only if you select “include additional contacts of recipients (CC).”
