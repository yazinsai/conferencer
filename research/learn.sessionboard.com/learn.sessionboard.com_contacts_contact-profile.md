# The contact profile

The Contact Profile is your central hub for managing a contact’s information. Open it by selecting the pencil icon to the left of a record in the **Contacts** or **Speakers** module. It’s organized into five tabs.

- [Profile](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/contact-profile#tab-panel-16)
- [Notes](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/contact-profile#tab-panel-17)
- [Connections](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/contact-profile#tab-panel-18)
- [Files](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/contact-profile#tab-panel-19)
- [Activity](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/contact-profile#tab-panel-20)

A quick view of the contact’s involvement. Expand for full detail:

- **Contact fields** — name, bio, photo, role, email/SMS options, portal task access
- **Address & social links**
- **Global attributes & custom fields**
- **Portal tasks** — progress on registration, hotel, speaker agreement, etc.
- **Additional contacts** — linked assistants or collaborators

Internal notes about the contact — **not visible to the contact**.

The events, sessions, and segments the contact is involved in.

All files associated with the contact and their sessions.

All communications sent to the contact, plus any changes made to their record.
