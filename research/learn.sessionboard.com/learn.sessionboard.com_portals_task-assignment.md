# Assign tasks to contacts and groups

* * *

You can assign tasks to individual records directly from the Contacts, Sponsors, or Exhibitors modules in just a few clicks. Here’s how:

## Option 1: Assign a Task to a Single Record

[Section titled “Option 1: Assign a Task to a Single Record”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/task-assignment#option-1-assign-a-task-to-a-single-record)

1. Navigate to the module you need to assign tasks through and locate the record (i.e., contact, sponsor, exhibitor) that needs the task assigned to them.
2. In the task column you want (for example `Speaker Agreement` or `Update Contact Information`), click the plus icon. If the task column is not in your table, add it with **Show/Hide Fields** — see [Dashboard views](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/dashboard-views).

![Option 1: Assign a Task to a Single Record](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/d76094e2-image-png-Feb-03-2026-09-35-21-9132-PM.png)

3. A pop-up will appear as a confirmation before the task is assigned. Select Assign to proceed.

![A pop-up will appear as a confirmation before the task is assigned](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/57ed1a5d-image-png-Feb-03-2026-09-36-27-0482-PM.png)

4. Click on the task again, and a new pop-up will appear. Select Options. This will open the same assignment modal as our portals, allowing admins to assign a due date and note whether the task is required.

![Click on the task again, and a new pop-up will appear](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/13444d66-image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-20-00-0492-PM.png)

![Click on the task again, and a new pop-up will appear](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/8196a919-image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-20-08-6382-PM.png)

## Option 2: Assign a Task in Bulk to One or More Records

[Section titled “Option 2: Assign a Task in Bulk to One or More Records”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/task-assignment#option-2-assign-a-task-in-bulk-to-one-or-more-records)

1. Navigate to the module you need to assign your tasks. Select the checkbox to the left of each record you’d like to assign the task to.
2. Once at least one record is selected, the bulk action menu will appear at the top. Click on the Assign button and choose to either assign a Task, Forms, or File Requests from the dropdown menu.

![Option 2: Assign a Task in Bulk to One or More Records](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/5206ae5a-undefined-Feb-03-2026-09-45-38-1052-PM.png)

3. In the assignment pop-up modal, choose the task(s) you want to assign by selecting the checkbox to the left of the task name. Once selected, click Next.

![In the assignment pop-up modal](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/6932b64c-image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-21-34-4493-PM.png)

4. The pop-up modal will update with an opportunity to assign due dates, note if the new assignment(s) are required, and if you’d like the assignment to close once complete. Click Assign when complete.

![The pop-up modal will update with an opportunity to assign due dates](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/d936d3f4-image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-21-46-3573-PM.png)

## Unassign Tasks

[Section titled “Unassign Tasks”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/task-assignment#unassign-tasks)

1. Navigate to the module you need to assign tasks through and locate the record (i.e. contact, session, sponsor, exhibitor) that needs the task assigned to them.
2. In the desired task column (e.g., Speaker Agreement, Update Contact Information), click the task icon to unassign.

![Unassign Tasks](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/20081ee8-image-png-Feb-17-2026-07-23-12-3875-PM.png)
NOTE: Tasks assigned via the portal can not be unassigned from a record.
