# Field types: session, people & group

When you build forms in Sessionboard, data is collected and stored in three distinct areas depending on the field type you select.

## Text vs. text area

[Section titled “Text vs. text area”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/concepts/field-types#text-vs-text-area)

| Field type | Character limit | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Text** | 255 | Single words or one-sentence responses |
| **Text area** | 5,000 | Longer responses of three or more sentences |

You can customize the character limit within these ranges for both field types.

## Session fields

[Section titled “Session fields”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/concepts/field-types#session-fields)

Capture information about the **session itself** when building session submission forms (under _Session Information_).

- Examples: title, description, tags, tracks, location.
- Data appears in the **Sessions** module.

## Speaker / individual fields

[Section titled “Speaker / individual fields”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/concepts/field-types#speaker--individual-fields)

Store **speaker contact profile** data. Used in session submission forms ( _Speaker Information_), intake forms ( _Contact Information_), and portal forms.

- Examples: name, email, social handles, address, bio, photo.
- Data appears in the **Contacts** module.

## Group fields

[Section titled “Group fields”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/concepts/field-types#group-fields)

Capture **organizational** information. Used on sponsor/exhibitor intake forms ( _Group Information_) and portal forms.

- Examples: name, description, address, social handles, logo, website.
- Data appears in the **Sponsors** or **Exhibitors** module.
