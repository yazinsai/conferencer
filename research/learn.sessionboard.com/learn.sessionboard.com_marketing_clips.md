# Clips

Clips turn a long recording into short videos built for social. AI proposes the moments worth cutting, you adjust the boundaries, and export in the aspect ratio and caption style you need.

## Create a clip

[Section titled “Create a clip”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/clips#create-a-clip)

Clips are cut from a recording, so start in Media.

Open a recording with a finished transcript

Go to **Marketing → Media** and open the item. Clip suggestions need a completed transcript.

Generate suggestions

Under **Suggested clips**, click **Generate**. AI proposes around six moments.

Preview and adjust

Check each suggested range and adjust the start and end points. You can also set your own in and out points instead of using a suggestion.

Export

Click **Export** and choose the aspect ratio — original, 9:16 for vertical, or 1:1 for square — plus a caption style: Clean, Bold, or Karaoke. Rendering runs in the background and shows **Rendering…** until it’s done.

Finished clips appear under **Exported clips** on the recording and in **Media → Clips**.

## The clips library

[Section titled “The clips library”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/clips#the-clips-library)

**Media → Clips** lists your exported clips. Opening a clip takes you to its source recording, where you can adjust and re-export.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/clips#faq)

How many suggestions do I get?

Around six per run, and you can generate again.

Why can't I generate clips on this recording?

It has no finished transcript. Suggestions are chosen from transcript content, so transcription has to complete first.

Can I add my own captions or branding?

Caption style is chosen at export. Logo watermarking and clip styling come from your [brand kit](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/brand-kits).

How long does export take?

It renders in the background — minutes for a short clip. The card shows **Rendering…** until it’s ready.
