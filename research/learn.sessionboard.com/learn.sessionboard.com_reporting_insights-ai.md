# Insights: AI reports & dashboards

Insights is Sessionboard’s reporting engine — create, save, share, and schedule reports with AI-powered query generation and data analysis.

## Three ways to build a report

[Section titled “Three ways to build a report”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#three-ways-to-build-a-report)

- [Describe it (AI)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#tab-panel-21)
- [Builder (no code)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#tab-panel-22)
- [SbQL editor (advanced)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#tab-panel-23)

Type a question in plain English — e.g., _“Report all accepted speakers”_ — and click **Generate Report**. Insights writes an **SbQL** query and shows a preview with a plain-language explanation before running. Suggestion chips offer common queries, and the AI can surface anomalies (e.g., sessions without assigned speakers).

Visually configure **Columns**, **Filters**, **Group By**, and **Sort By**.

Write [SbQL](https://learn.sessionboard.com/integrations/api-tokens) directly for precise control, using the **Schema Explorer** as a field reference.

## Run & export

[Section titled “Run & export”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#run--export)

Results display in a table you can search, group, and paginate. **Export** to CSV or XLSX.

## Save, share & schedule

[Section titled “Save, share & schedule”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#save-share--schedule)

- **Save** — named reports appear under **Custom Reports**.
- **Share** — create secure links that show **live data** (not snapshots), with optional password, expiration, and view limits. Each link exposes multiple formats: public link, CSV, **Google Sheets `=IMPORTDATA()` formula**, JSON, and XLSX.
- **Schedule** — automated Daily/Weekly/Monthly email delivery to up to 5 recipients (Sessionboard accounts not required); links expire after 7 days.

## PII mode

[Section titled “PII mode”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#pii-mode)

Toggle **PII Mode** to mask names and email addresses while running and reviewing reports.

## Permissions

[Section titled “Permissions”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#permissions)

Control access under **Event Team → Permissions → Data & Insights**: view, create, share, and schedule reports; manage dashboards; and connect AI assistants.

## Dashboards

[Section titled “Dashboards”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/reporting/insights-ai#dashboards)

Build dashboards where each widget has its own query and can pull from any module (Sessions, Contacts, Evaluations…), so you can mix modules across widgets.
