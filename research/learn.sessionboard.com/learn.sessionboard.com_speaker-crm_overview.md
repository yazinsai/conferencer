# Speaker CRM overview

The Speaker CRM keeps your speakers and event contacts in one place, so you can search, invite, and activate them across your entire event marketing portfolio.

## What the CRM gives you

[Section titled “What the CRM gives you”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/overview#what-the-crm-gives-you)

A CRM (customer relationship management) provides deep transparency into your network, improves communication, and reduces the time it takes to identify the right speakers — across conferences, field events, webinars, case studies, and more.

## Organization navigation

[Section titled “Organization navigation”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/overview#organization-navigation)

Dashboard

Org-wide metrics: total events, contacts, accepted and returning speakers, plus quick-start tools.

[Pipeline\\
Kanban sourcing workflow from identification to confirmation.](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/pipeline)

Directory

All contacts — speakers, moderators, chairpersons, sponsor/exhibitor contacts, submitters.

Segments

Dynamic segments and curated static lists.

Fields

Create and manage attributes that categorize contact data.

History

Every email sent by your event team.

## Dashboard components

[Section titled “Dashboard components”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/overview#dashboard-components)

- **Speaker Engagement Flow** — the speaker journey from outreach through acceptance (email counts, acceptances, multi-event associations).
- **Top Companies** — organizations with the most associated contacts.
- **Speaker Source** — how records were added (submission forms, applications, manual/import).
- **Areas of Focus** — contacts by topic/expertise (uses the **Tags** field).
- **Contacts by Region** — contact locations globally (uses City/State/Zip/Country).

## Settings

[Section titled “Settings”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/overview#settings)

- **Details** — name, slug, logo, banner image
- **Team** — manage members with org/event access
- **Email templates** — org-level templates for contacts
- **Integrations** — manage event integrations
- **API tokens** — connect third-party systems via the [Open API](https://learn.sessionboard.com/integrations/api-tokens)
