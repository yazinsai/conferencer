# What the New and Returning badges mean

## TL;DR

[Section titled “TL;DR”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#tldr)

- New = the contact was added to your organization’s database in the last 30 days.
- Returning = not new, and the contact is related to at least one past event in your org.
- No badge = neither condition applies.

* * *

## How it works

[Section titled “How it works”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#how-it-works)

Badges appear on the contact’s profile avatar to help admins quickly segment outreach.

### New

[Section titled “New”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#new)

- Shown when Created Date ≤ 30 days ago (rolling window).
- Purpose: highlight truly new records for onboarding/welcome campaigns.

![New](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/34e463c4-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-50-12-0860-PM.png)

### Returning

[Section titled “Returning”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#returning)

- If the contact is older than 30 days and has historical relationship to an existing/past event (e.g., speaker, submitter, sponsor, exhibitor), the badge shows Returning.

![Returning](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/1c6a2ad6-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-47-02-9268-PM.png)

### No badge

[Section titled “No badge”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#no-badge)

- If the contact is older than 30 days and has no prior event relationship, no badge is displayed.

![No badge](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/0bf51c21-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-47-54-7357-PM.png)

* * *

## Practical examples

[Section titled “Practical examples”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#practical-examples)

- You imported a speaker yesterday → New
- A speaker you worked with last year gets re-added to an event today → Returning
- A contact created 45 days ago who hasn’t been tied to any event previously → No badge

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-does-the-new-returning-badge-on-a-contacts-profile-indicate#faq)

**Q: Is “New” based on accepted sessions?**
A: No. It’s strictly based on the contact’s Created Date (last 30 days).

**Q: Where do I see the badge?**
A: On the contact’s profile picture within the contact record.

**Q: What happens after 30 days?**
A: The New badge automatically drops. If the contact has past event ties, they’ll show Returning; otherwise, no badge.

**Q: What if a contact is edited or reassigned?**
A: Edits don’t change the Created Date. Badges follow the rules above.
