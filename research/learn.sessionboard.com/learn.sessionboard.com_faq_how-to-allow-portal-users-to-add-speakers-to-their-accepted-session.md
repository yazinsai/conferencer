# Let portal users add speakers

Sessionboard offers two ways to collect additional speakers after a submission form has closed:

## Option 1: Re-open your submission form and create a portal task

[Section titled “Option 1: Re-open your submission form and create a portal task”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#option-1-re-open-your-submission-form-and-create-a-portal-task)

### Step 1: Locate your submission form

[Section titled “Step 1: Locate your submission form”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#step-1-locate-your-submission-form)

1. From the **Dashboard** module, find the session submission form.
2. Click **Manage**.

### Step 2: Adjust the form close date

[Section titled “Step 2: Adjust the form close date”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#step-2-adjust-the-form-close-date)

1. Go to **Form Settings**.
2. Update the **Close Date** to a future date.

This re-opens the form, allowing sessions to be edited until the new deadline.

![Step 2: Adjust the form close date](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e1064a2e-image-png-Feb-24-2026-08-59-09-2976-PM.png)

### Step 3: Notify speakers via a portal task

[Section titled “Step 3: Notify speakers via a portal task”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#step-3-notify-speakers-via-a-portal-task)

1. Create a portal task to let your speakers know they can add additional speakers to their sessions.
   - Use clear task instructions such as: “Add additional speakers by editing your original submission form.”
   - Include a link to the [View & edit your submission](https://learn.sessionboard.com/participants/edit-submission) help article for portal users.
2. Enable **View Session Submission Form** in your portal settings. This ensures users see a direct link back to the form, making it easy to complete the update.

![Step 3: Notify speakers via a portal task](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/4871eecd-image-png-Feb-24-2026-08-59-53-3712-PM.png)

Using this route, additional speakers can be added to a session without assistance from admins.

## Option 2: Create a session portal form for additional speakers

[Section titled “Option 2: Create a session portal form for additional speakers”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#option-2-create-a-session-portal-form-for-additional-speakers)

If you prefer more control over how new speakers are added, you can build a session portal form specifically for collecting additional speaker details. Unlike reopening the submission form, this method routes new information to the admin team for review before adding speakers to a session.

### Step 1: Build a session portal form

[Section titled “Step 1: Build a session portal form”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#step-1-build-a-session-portal-form)

1. From the **Portal** module, click **Forms**.
2. Create a new session portal form. Learn how in [Create & assign portal forms](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/create-assign-forms).
3. Add fields to collect the required speaker information, such as first name, last name, email address, and any additional custom fields your team requires (e.g., company, bio, headshot).

### Step 2: Configure the form for portal users

[Section titled “Step 2: Configure the form for portal users”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#step-2-configure-the-form-for-portal-users)

1. Assign the form to your portal in **Assign Items** so it is visible to session participants.
2. If the option to add additional speakers should be limited to specific sessions, apply a filter to the form.

### Step 3: Review and process new speakers

[Section titled “Step 3: Review and process new speakers”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-allow-portal-users-to-add-speakers-to-their-accepted-session#step-3-review-and-process-new-speakers)

1. When a form is submitted, the new speaker information is viewable by clicking **View Submissions**.

![Step 3: Review and process new speakers](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/5d24e4c9-image-png-Feb-24-2026-09-01-10-3820-PM.png)

2. Admins can review the details before manually creating the new contact and attaching them to the appropriate session.


Using this route, portal users have a simple way to submit additional speaker requests, and your team retains control over which speakers are added to the event.
