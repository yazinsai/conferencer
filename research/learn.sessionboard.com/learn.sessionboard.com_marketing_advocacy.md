# Advocacy campaigns

Advocacy gives every speaker, sponsor, and exhibitor their own branded share card and tracked link. They post in one tap from a Creator console, and you see who shared, what got clicked, and which advocates drive the most traffic — without chasing anyone for a screenshot.

## Before you start

[Section titled “Before you start”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#before-you-start)

- A **register link** — the URL your shares point at. Sessionboard prefills your event website if it’s set under **Settings → Event Details**.
- **Speakers, sponsors, or exhibitors** on the event, with email addresses if you plan to notify them.
- Your event branding, so the generated share cards carry your logo and colors.

## Who can share

[Section titled “Who can share”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#who-can-share)

You pick the audience when you build the campaign.

| Audience | Who it is | Requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Speakers** | Your presenters sharing that they’re speaking | Nothing extra |
| **Groups** | Sponsor and exhibitor companies | Exhibitor Pro or Sponsor Pro |

## Launch a campaign

[Section titled “Launch a campaign”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#launch-a-campaign)

Go to **Marketing → Advocacy** and click **New campaign**. This opens Quick Launch, which is how campaigns are created — the longer campaign wizard is for editing one that already exists.

Build

Choose who’s sharing, then pick a format — **Video** for an animated share card, or **Image** for a static graphic. Add your **Register link** (must start with `https://`), and optionally a campaign name and promo code.

The card builds itself from your event, so there’s no design step.

Recipients

Choose **All contacts**, **By portal**, or **Select** to hand-pick people, then click **Continue to launch**.

Landing

Edit the branded interstitial people see before they’re forwarded on — layout, font, and colors, with a live preview of the `/r/` link.

Launch

Review the summary and click **Activate campaign**.

Notify your advocates

On the success screen, click **Notify** to email everyone their personal link to the Creator console. You can edit the subject and message; the default subject is “You’re featured at _your event_ — here’s your share link.”

Nothing reaches your advocates until you send this, so don’t skip it.

## How the links work

[Section titled “How the links work”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#how-the-links-work)

Every recipient gets their own short link in the form `https://sessionboard.com/r/` plus an eight-character code. When someone clicks it, Sessionboard records the click, applies your UTM parameters and promo code, and forwards them to your destination — optionally through your branded landing page first.

Because each link is per person, shares and clicks attribute back to the individual advocate rather than to the campaign as a whole.

## Read the analytics

[Section titled “Read the analytics”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#read-the-analytics)

Open the **Analytics** tab for the whole event, or a single campaign for its own numbers.

- **Advocates** — how many assigned people have activated, shown as activated over assigned
- **Shares** — total share actions across channels
- **Clicks** — total clicks, with unique visitors counted separately by consent cookie
- **Advocacy funnel** — Assigned, Opened, Shared, Clicked
- **Channel mix**, **Top advocates**, **Geography**, and a per-campaign breakdown

The **Activity** tab is the raw feed, filterable by Opens, Shares, and Clicks.

## Across events

[Section titled “Across events”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#across-events)

**Advocacy** at the organization level rolls every event’s campaigns into one read-only view. Campaigns are always created inside an event, so use the org view for reporting rather than setup.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy#faq)

Do advocates need a Sessionboard login?

No. They get a magic link to the Creator console, which needs no account.

Which channels can they share to?

LinkedIn is the default channel on new campaigns. Additional channels can be configured per campaign.

Can I change a campaign after launching it?

Yes. Open the campaign and click **Edit campaign** to walk through audience, basics, recipients, templates and copy, landing page, and launch.

Why do my advocates show as assigned but never activated?

Assigned means they’re on the campaign; activated means they’ve opened their Creator console. If nobody has activated, check that you sent the notify email and that recipients have valid email addresses.
