# Reset password (user instructions)

Forgot your password?

1. Go to [https://app.sessionboard.com/forgot](https://app.sessionboard.com/forgot) or select Forgot password? on the sign-in page.

![Reset password (user instructions)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/db8442c7-image-png-Jan-28-2026-09-56-45-9216-PM.png)

2. Enter the email address of the user who forgot their password.

![Enter the email address of the user who forgot their password](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e5c8b5ec-image-png-Jan-28-2026-09-56-59-3193-PM.png)

3. Click Send Reset Link. The user will receive an email with instructions on resetting their password. The email will come from “ [no-reply@sessionboard.com](mailto:no-reply@sessionboard.com)”.

Sample Reset Password EmailSubject Line: Reset Your Sessionboard Password To Access Your Account
