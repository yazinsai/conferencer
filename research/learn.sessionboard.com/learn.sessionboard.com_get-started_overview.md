# What is Sessionboard?

Sessionboard is a next-generation **speaker and content management platform** that helps event organizers run their entire speaker and session program — from call for papers through to a published agenda and post-event content.

Schedule sessions with a drag-and-drop calendar and conflict alerts.](https://learn.sessionboard.com/sessions/agenda) [Manage speakers\\

## Core capabilities

[Section titled “Core capabilities”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/get-started/overview#core-capabilities)

- **Call for papers** — consolidate speaker submissions for centralized review and management.
- **Session evaluation & grading** — smart voting to surface your best content.
- **Drag-and-drop agenda** — keep scheduling flexible as speaker availability changes.
- **Conflict detection** — catch overlapping sessions and double-booked participants automatically.
- **Email & SMS** — reach groups with custom templates and full send history.
- **Content & media** — centralize speaker bios, files, and event materials.
- **Document requests** — collect insurance certificates, contracts, and required documentation.
