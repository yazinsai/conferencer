# Brand kits

A brand kit holds your visual identity and your writing voice in one place. Set it up once and every AI draft, carousel, and clip picks it up — so generated content sounds like you rather than like generic conference copy.

## What’s in a kit

[Section titled “What’s in a kit”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/brand-kits#whats-in-a-kit)

- **Brand voice** — a summary, tone words, signature vocabulary, things to do, things to avoid, and example passages
- **Visual kit** — colors, fonts, logos, and backgrounds
- **Output rules** — default hashtags, link and UTM source, handles to tag, a default call to action, emoji usage, reading level, boilerplate or disclaimer text, words to avoid, and clip settings for caption style, logo watermark, and aspect ratios

Kits live at the organization level. Events choose which kit to use.

## Create a kit

[Section titled “Create a kit”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/brand-kits#create-a-kit)

Go to **Marketing → Brand** and click **New kit**, then give it a name — “Main” or “Awards 2026”, for example.

Import your visuals

Use **Import from URL** with your website and Sessionboard pulls colors and fonts from the page. Add logos and backgrounds by uploading them.

Add a voice source

Under **Brand voice**, add a URL that represents how you write — an About page or a strong blog post works well.

Bake the voice

Click **Bake voice**. Sessionboard reads the source and fills in summary, tone, vocabulary, do, avoid, and examples. It takes a moment, and you can edit every field afterwards.

Set output rules

Add hashtags, a default link and UTM source, a call to action, and any words to avoid. Set clip caption style and aspect ratios if you produce video.

Your first kit becomes the organization default automatically. Everything auto-saves.

## Apply a kit to an event

[Section titled “Apply a kit to an event”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/brand-kits#apply-a-kit-to-an-event)

Go to **Marketing → Brand** inside the event and choose the kit. Events without an explicit kit inherit the organization default, and the badge tells you which is in effect.

From the kit menu you can **Set as org default** or return an event to **Use org default**.

## What uses a kit

[Section titled “What uses a kit”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/brand-kits#what-uses-a-kit)

- **Create** — voice and output rules are prepended to every draft
- **Carousels** — colors, fonts, and logos
- **Clips** — caption style, watermark, aspect ratios
- **Advocacy** — share card templates

Deleting a kit isn’t destructive to those surfaces; they fall back to the default.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/brand-kits#faq)

Why is Bake voice disabled?

You need at least one URL source first. Add one under Brand voice and the button becomes available.

Can I write the voice by hand instead?

Yes. Every field is editable whether or not you’ve baked one.

Can different events have different kits?

Yes — that’s the point of naming them. Assign per event; anything unassigned uses the org default.

Are these the same as print document branding?

No. [Print agendas](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/print-agendas) use a separate branding setup under **Settings → Branding**.
