# Posts

Posts is the library of written work you’ve drafted in Create. Filter by format, search across everything, and click any card to reopen it for editing.

## What’s in it

[Section titled “What’s in it”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/posts#whats-in-it)

Everything drafted in [Create](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/create) except video projects, which live with your [clips](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/clips). Filter by **LinkedIn**, **Blog**, **White paper**, or **Carousel**, or search by name.

Inside an event you see that event’s posts. At the organization level you see everything across every event.

## What it doesn’t do

[Section titled “What it doesn’t do”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/posts#what-it-doesnt-do)

Posts is a directory, not a workflow. There’s no approve step, no scheduling, and no publishing — clicking a card takes you back into Create, where the editing and export tools live.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/posts#faq)

Why is a post missing?

Video projects are excluded by design — look under Clips. Otherwise check whether you’re in an event view, which filters to that event.

Can I schedule a post from here?

No. Schedule the cadence in [Content plan](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/content-plan) and distribute through [Advocacy](https://learn.sessionboard.com/marketing/advocacy).

How do I delete one?

Delete the project from Create.
