# AI agenda builder

The AI agenda builder drafts a schedule from your sessions, rooms, and event dates. Drafts are separate from your live agenda — you compare the two, accept changes individually or all at once, and nothing reaches attendees until you commit.

## Configure event criteria first (recommended)

[Section titled “Configure event criteria first (recommended)”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/studio/ai-agenda-builder#configure-event-criteria-first-recommended)

Criteria are the scheduling rules the AI follows, such as keeping a track in one room or avoiding two sessions by the same speaker at once. Setting them at the event level means they auto-populate every new draft, so you are not retyping them each time. You can still adjust them per draft.

Go to **Settings → Library → Criteria** in the left sidebar.

![Event criteria configured at the event level](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/dde9e2ab-image-png-May-25-2026-06-26-22-7514-PM.png)

![The Criteria library in the left sidebar](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/45caae93-image-png-May-25-2026-06-29-17-4618-PM.png)

## Create a draft agenda

[Section titled “Create a draft agenda”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/studio/ai-agenda-builder#create-a-draft-agenda)

Start a new draft

Go to **Program → Agenda → Drafts** and click **Create New Draft**.

![The Drafts button on the Agenda page](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/9594d38c-image-png-May-25-2026-06-31-36-0781-PM.png)

Setup

Confirm your event dates, session count, and available rooms, then click **Next**.

Settings

Set the **Day Start** and **Day End** times, and choose which **Session Statuses** to include, such as Accepted or Accept Queue.

![Day start and end times with session status selection](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/513b10c1-image-png-May-25-2026-06-35-24-2388-PM.png)

Turn on **Ignore Existing Times/Rooms** if you want the AI to reschedule everything from scratch rather than working around what is already placed.

![The Ignore Existing Times and Rooms toggle](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/66a5d04a-image-png-May-25-2026-06-36-06-6476-PM.png)

Rooms

Pick the rooms this draft can use, then click **Next**.

Rules

Add the criteria that guide scheduling. Type a rule and click **Add Criteria**, or pull one in from your **Event Criteria** or the AI-recommended **Suggested** list.

![Adding scheduling criteria to a draft](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/c5882e9a-image-png-May-25-2026-06-37-10-0825-PM.png)

![Reordering criteria to set priority](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/5189a0ea-image-png-May-25-2026-06-38-25-0853-PM.png)

Generate

Click **Next & Generate Schedule**. You will see **Agenda Generated** when the draft is ready.

![The Agenda Generated confirmation](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/245b4137-image-png-May-25-2026-06-39-07-0821-PM.png)

From there, choose **Review Agenda** or **See What Changed**.

![Choosing between Review Agenda and See What Changed](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/0c32700e-image-png-May-25-2026-06-42-03-1706-PM.png)

## Review and commit a draft

[Section titled “Review and commit a draft”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/studio/ai-agenda-builder#review-and-commit-a-draft)

Open the draft

Go to **Program → Agenda → Drafts** and open the draft you want.

![The list of draft agendas](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/141e344f-image-png-May-25-2026-06-43-28-3616-PM.png)

Compare against your live agenda

Click **View & Commit Changes**.

![Comparing a draft against the live agenda](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/a29988ef-image-png-May-25-2026-06-42-50-5729-PM.png)

Accept the changes you want

Review each proposed change — time, room, and so on — and click **Accept**, or use **Accept All Changes**.

![Accepting individual proposed changes](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/9dd5807e-image-png-May-25-2026-06-44-50-8138-PM.png)

Commit

Click **Commit Changes** to publish the accepted updates to your live agenda.

![The Commit Changes button](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/2722c706-image-png-May-25-2026-06-45-39-3173-PM.png)

Need a different result? Use **Edit Draft** to re-run with different settings, rooms, or criteria instead of starting over.

## Delete or duplicate a draft

[Section titled “Delete or duplicate a draft”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/studio/ai-agenda-builder#delete-or-duplicate-a-draft)

Go to **Program → Agenda → Drafts**, click the ellipsis menu next to a draft, and choose **Delete** or **Duplicate**.

![The ellipsis menu with Delete and Duplicate](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/9feceba0-image-png-May-25-2026-06-47-34-2170-PM.png)

Deleting a draft does not affect your live agenda unless you already committed changes from it.

## FAQ

[Section titled “FAQ”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/studio/ai-agenda-builder#faq)

Why didn't the AI schedule all my sessions?

The AI only schedules sessions matching the statuses you selected in the Settings step, such as Accepted or Accept Queue. Sessions outside those statuses, or ones that conflict with your criteria, are left out. Widen your status filter or look for overly restrictive rules.

Does committing a draft overwrite my existing agenda?

No. Committing applies only the changes you explicitly accepted. Anything you did not accept is ignored.

Can I run multiple drafts at the same time?

Yes. Drafts are independent, so you can build several configurations and compare them before committing any.

What happens if I delete a draft?

Nothing happens to your live agenda, as long as you have not already committed changes from that draft. It just removes the draft from your list.

Can I re-run a draft with different settings?

Yes. Open the draft and use **Edit Draft** to re-run with updated settings, rooms, or criteria.
