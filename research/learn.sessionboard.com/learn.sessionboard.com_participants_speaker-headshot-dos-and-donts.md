# Speaker headshot do's and don'ts

Your headshot appears on the event agenda, program site, and any speaker embeds, so it’s worth submitting a photo that shows your best side. Follow these simple tips.

![three approved examples and three to avoid](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/d4a5a314-Headshot-Dos-and-Donts.jpg)

**Do**

- Face the camera and smile naturally.
- Use a well-lit photo.
- Use a neutral background.

**Don’t**

- Use uneven lighting.
- Share the spotlight — you should be the only person in the photo.
- Use busy or distracting backgrounds.

See [What is the recommended size for contact headshots?](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/what-is-the-recommended-size-for-contact-headshots) for image dimensions and file requirements.
