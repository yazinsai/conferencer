# Email moderators and chairpersons

If you’re looking to communicate with Moderators or Chairpersons, it’s important to know that emails can be sent through both the Sessions & the Contacts modules.

## Using sessions module

[Section titled “Using sessions module”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-email-moderators-chairpersons#using-sessions-module)

To email Moderators or Chairpersons from the Sessions Module follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to the Sessions > Submissions module:

![Using Sessions Module](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/37366593-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-25-09-8435-PM.png)

2. Select session & Send Emails

![Select session & Send Emails](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/1acf3d20-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-25-42-4382-PM.png)

3. You can now select which contacts to send the email to, including moderators & chairpersons, or only to them:

![You can now select which contacts to send the email to, including moderators & chairpersons, or only to them](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/b2c2d8b2-image-png-Feb-17-2026-06-43-30-0811-PM.png)

## Using contacts module

[Section titled “Using contacts module”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-email-moderators-chairpersons#using-contacts-module)

To email Moderators or Chairpersons from the Contacts Module follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to the Contacts module & access the needed subsection:

![Using Contacts Module](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/43cb0cae-image-png-Feb-09-2026-07-26-11-7499-PM.png)

2. Using the checkbox to the left of the relevant contacts (Moderators or Chairpersons), select the contacts you wish to email and click the button Send > Send Emails at the top of your page.

![Using the checkbox to the left of the relevant contacts (Moderators or Chairpersons)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/8ffe8ec8-undefined-Feb-17-2026-06-45-00-8568-PM.png)

3. Within the pop-up, compose and send your message.

## Recommended Approach

[Section titled “Recommended Approach”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/how-to-email-moderators-chairpersons#recommended-approach)

We recommend instructing Moderators and Chairpersons to log in to their portal to view their assigned sessions and relevant session details within the My Sessions widget.

![Recommended Approach](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/ea35dbe7-Screenshot-2025-06-05-at-2.16.10-PM.png)

If you want to include a direct link to the portal in your email, use the \[PORTAL\_LINK\] merge tag where applicable.

![Recommended Approach](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e5cd917d-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-21-26-8293-PM.png)
