# Portals 101

Portals give contacts (speakers, moderators, chairpersons) and groups (sponsors, exhibitors) a centralized hub to access event information and complete tasks.

## Four steps to launch

[Section titled “Four steps to launch”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/portals-101#four-steps-to-launch)

Create portals & assign filters

Every event includes default portals: **Default People**, **Default Exhibitor**, and **Default Sponsor**. Create **custom portals** to tailor the experience by criteria such as contact role, session type, or sponsor tier. To show tasks only to accepted speakers, disable **Always Show Tasks**.

Create & assign tasks & resources

**Task types:**

- **Task** — a general action item with a deadline (e.g., “Register for the event”)
- **File request** — requires an upload (e.g., “Upload contract”)
- **Form** — collects structured responses (e.g., “Provide headshot & bio”)

**Resources:**

- **Files** — static, downloadable documents (e.g., a slide template)
- **Wiki pages** — dynamic, editable pages (e.g., a “Know Before You Go” guide)

Configure settings

Control session/task/contact visibility and reminder emails — including **Always Show Tasks**, **Extend Task Deadlines** (set a **Final Deadline** — the number of days after the original due date before tasks are locked; defaults to 7), **Manage Profile**, **Manage Related Sessions & Participants**, and **Send Weekly Digest Email**.

Customize appearance

Set a welcome message, accent color, logo, and background image per portal. Control which **contact** and **session** fields users can view or edit via **Show/Hide Fields** (lock to make view-only, or hide entirely). Click **Save**.

## Participation sections

[Section titled “Participation sections”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/portals-101#participation-sections)

When [participant acceptance](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speakers/speaker-acceptance) is on, the portal splits each person’s sessions into sections. You control the grouping and the wording per portal, in **Portals → \[portal\] → Configuration**, under **Participation sections**. The controls appear only when acceptance is enabled and the portal is set to show sessions.

By default a participant sees three sections:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited Sessions** | Sessions they have been invited to but not yet answered |
| **My Submissions** | Submissions they made |
| **Confirmed Participation** | Everything they have accepted |

### Show a separate Confirmed Participation section

[Section titled “Show a separate Confirmed Participation section”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/portals-101#show-a-separate-confirmed-participation-section)

On by default. Turn it off to collapse to two sections — accepted submissions stay in **My Submissions**, and answered invitations stay in **Invited Sessions**. Useful when a third section is more structure than your participants need.

### Show subsessions in My Submissions

[Section titled “Show subsessions in My Submissions”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/portals-101#show-subsessions-in-my-submissions)

On by default. Turn it off and **My Submissions** lists the parent submission only, with subsessions visible once the submitter opens the record. Worth doing when a single submission carries many subsessions and the list becomes hard to scan.

**Invited Sessions** always shows the specific subsession a participant is on, whichever way this is set.

### Section titles

[Section titled “Section titles”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/portals/portals-101#section-titles)

Rename any of the three headings, up to 100 characters each. Leave a field blank to keep the default. If your portal has multi-language enabled, custom titles are auto-translated along with the rest of the portal header copy.

The **Confirmed** title field is hidden when you have turned that section off, since there is nothing to name.
