# View a wiki page in your portal

1. Log into your portal
2. Click on the button Resources at the top of your portal.

![View a wiki page in your portal](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e3401bc5-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.41.27-AM.png)

3. From the Resources widget, select a Wiki Page you would like to view.

![View a wiki page in your portal](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/2fda45ce-Screenshot-2025-02-17-at-11.42.51-AM.png)
