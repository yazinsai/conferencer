# Bulk edit fields

To edit one field within a module (contacts, speakers, session, sponsor, or exhibitor) for multiple records, consider editing in bulk.

![Bulk edit fields](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/4e3f12f0-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-19-31-1655-PM.png)

2. Click the button “Edit” at the top of your page and select the field you wish to edit within the dropdown menu.

![Click the button “Edit” at the top of your page and select the field you wish to edit within the dropdown menu](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/4b29b0f1-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-21-20-9466-PM.png)

3. Once complete, click the “Save” button at the bottom of the pop-up window.
