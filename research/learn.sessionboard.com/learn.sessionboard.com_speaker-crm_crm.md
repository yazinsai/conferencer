# Speaker CRM: communications

This guide applies only to organizations that have the Speaker CRM enabled.

Email templates and email sending from the CRM transform how organizations communicate, making it faster, more targeted, and more impactful. By combining the efficiency of templates with the CRM’s data-driven capabilities, you can scale your outreach efforts while maintaining a personal and professional touch.

* * *

## Email Templates

[Section titled “Email Templates”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/crm#email-templates)

To create a template, navigate to Settings > Email Templates and click the “+ Add Template” button in the top-right corner of your screen.

There is no limit to the number of templates you can create. Templates will be ordered alphabetically (A-Z) by the template name.

![communications: Email Templates](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/7cb52e81-image-png-Mar-13-2026-09-53-26-3952-PM.png)

Within the pop-up window, customize your email to include:

- Template Name: Internal Name
- Type: Contacts or Awards
- Reply To: The email address that should receive replies to the email sent. We recommend using a shared inbox or an event-specific email address. This field only supports one email address.
- CC (optional): Include up to five (5) email addresses, separated by commas.
- BCC (optional): Include up to five (5) email addresses, separated by commas.
- Subject Line: The subject line of the email
- Message Body: Customize the body of the email with the use of merge tags to pull in dynamic contact details, as well as links. This will allow you to send emails in bulk with a personalized touch.

![Message Body](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/374c3ad5-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-07-46-2922-PM.png)

Edit, Delete, and Duplicate Templates

To manage the actions for an email template, select the three dots to the right of the template name.

![To manage the actions for an email template, select the three dots to the right of the template name](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/46819cfc-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-13-04-3184-PM.png)

Example Email Template

- Speaker Invitation
  - Purpose: Invite potential speakers and outline the next steps for how to submit a session proposal.

## Creating & sending emails

[Section titled “Creating & sending emails”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/crm#creating--sending-emails)

By default, all emails will be sent from “ [no-reply@sessionboard.com](mailto:no-reply@sessionboard.com)”. If you would like to have a customized email domain, please contact [support@sessionboard.com](mailto:support@sessionboard.com).

1. Within the CRM > Directory module, select the checkbox to the left of the contact(s) to email and select either the Communicate or Invite To Event buttons

![communications: Creating & Sending Emails](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/a7929521-image-png-Mar-13-2026-09-54-16-7694-PM.png)

1. Communicate: Create a custom email to tailor communications for specific contacts
2. Invite to event: Send an email inviting contacts to submit a session idea for an upcoming event.
3. Within the pop-up modal, indicate your email recipient (Who should receive this email?), a reply to email (replies sent to), and build your email subject line and message body (or insert a message template).

![Within the pop-up modal, indicate your email recipient (Who should receive this email?)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/7f1dd574-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-20-56-9309-PM.png)

3. Once you have built your email, select either the ‘Review’ button to preview what your email will look like before it is sent.
4. NOTE: Additional contacts will receive their own email and will not be copied on the email received by the main event contact (i.e. Speaker).
5. When you are ready to send your email, select the ‘Send Now’ button.

![When you are ready to send your email, select the ‘Send Now’ button](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e337c4d6-image-png-Feb-25-2026-02-22-23-0734-PM.png)

### Tabs (Email Campaigns & Sent Emails)

[Section titled “Tabs (Email Campaigns & Sent Emails)”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/speaker-crm/crm#tabs-email-campaigns--sent-emails)

![communications: Tabs](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/442658df-image-png-Mar-13-2026-09-54-52-9891-PM.png)

- CAMPAIGNS An email campaign is a coordinated series of emails sent to a targeted group of recipients with a specific purpose. Email campaigns might be used to notify speakers of important deadlines, share acceptance updates, or guide them through the submission process. Campaign performance is tracked using metrics such as open rates, click-through rates, and delivery status. Each row represents an individual email campaign with the following columns:
  - Subject Line: Title of the email (click to view campaign details).
  - Module: What module the campaign was sent from (i.e. Sessions, Contacts, Sponsors).
  - Total Recipients: Number of people the email was sent to.
  - Unique Opens: Number of recipients who opened the email.
  - Sent By: Sender’s name
  - Sent At: Date and time the email was sent.

Clicking a campaign subject opens a popup model with campaign details:

![Tabs (Email Campaigns & Sent Emails)](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/16ce5008-Screenshot-2025-04-21-at-1.12.21-PM.png)

- Sent By: Sender’s name.
- Sent At: Date and time sent.
- Recipients List: Link to view recipient(s).
- Selecting “view recipients” displays:
- Recipient Name
- Email Address
- Status: (e.g., Delivered)
- Reason: If undelivered, shows reason.
- Total Recipients / Unique Opens / Unique Clicks: Key engagement metrics.
- Preview Panel: Visual of the email content for reference.
- SENT EMAILS Displays individual sent email logs. Each entry on this page includes the following details:
  - Recipient: The name of the individual the email was sent to.
  - Email: The recipient’s email address.
  - Subject: The subject line of the email, with clickable links to preview the message.

![Subject: The subject line of the email, with clickable links to preview the message](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e70b88ea-Screenshot-2024-03-11-at-11_42_57-AM-1.png)

- Status: Indicates the current delivery status of the email (e.g., Delivered, Opened). Email Status Definitions:
  - Open - Successful delivery, the email has been opened by the recipient.
  - Clicked - Successful delivery, a link in the email has been clicked
  - Bounced - Not successful delivery, the incoming server rejected the email.
  - Spam - The email was delivered, but is in the recipient’s SPAM.
- Sent By: The name of the user who sent the email.
- Sent At: The date and time the email was sent.

The Emails page allows teams to track communication history, verify delivery, and troubleshoot email-related issues more efficiently. You can also search emails by recipient name or address using the search bar at the top of the page.
