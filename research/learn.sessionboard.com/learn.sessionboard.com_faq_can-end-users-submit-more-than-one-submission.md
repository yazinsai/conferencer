# Can participants submit more than one proposal?

## Yes, participants can submit more than one proposal for your event.

[Section titled “Yes, participants can submit more than one proposal for your event.”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/can-end-users-submit-more-than-one-submission#yes-participants-can-submit-more-than-one-proposal-for-your-event)

Once the first submission is complete, the participant navigates back to your submission form link to start a new one. If admins include it, the submitter also gets a link on the success page to submit another form.

![The success page with a link to submit another proposal](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e7f06e20-image-png-Feb-17-2026-09-22-19-5366-PM.png)

Submitting more than once creates duplicate contacts when the same speakers appear on each submission.

We recommend auditing once the submission form closes, to remove duplicates and confirm every submission has speakers assigned.

## Admins may limit the number of submissions tied to a submission form.

[Section titled “Admins may limit the number of submissions tied to a submission form.”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/faq/can-end-users-submit-more-than-one-submission#admins-may-limit-the-number-of-submissions-tied-to-a-submission-form)

Turn on **Set Submission Limit** in Form Settings to control how many submissions one submitter can make.

![The Set Submission Limit setting in Form Settings](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/5b6430b7-image-png-Feb-17-2026-09-22-47-2046-PM.png)
