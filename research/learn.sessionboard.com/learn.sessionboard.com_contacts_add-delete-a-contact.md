# Add & delete a contact

Before you can assign a speaker, sponsor, and/or exhibitor to a session, they must be added as a contact within the Contacts module first.

![Add & delete a contact](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/5a40473e-image-png-Feb-09-2026-08-15-25-1495-PM.png)

## How to add a contact

[Section titled “How to add a contact”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/add-delete-a-contact#how-to-add-a-contact)

1. Within the Contacts module, select the “Add Contact” button at the top right-hand corner of the screen.

![How to add a contact](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/22806670-image-png-Feb-02-2026-07-42-04-8325-PM.png)

2. Select the “Add new contact” button in the pop-up window.

![Select the “Add new contact” button in the pop-up window](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/9a3a2865-image-png-Feb-02-2026-07-42-57-7887-PM.png)

3. Add all of the contact’s information (i.e. name, phone number, address).
4. NOTE: First name, last name, and email address are required.
5. Click “Create Contact” once complete

You can control which fields appear in the Add Contact modal (and their order) via Settings → Record Settings → Layouts.

## How to delete a contact

[Section titled “How to delete a contact”](https://learn.sessionboard.com/contacts/add-delete-a-contact#how-to-delete-a-contact)

1. Within the Contacts module, select the box to the left of the contact record.
2. At the top of the page, select “Delete”.

![How to delete a contact](https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/d21bc9a7-image-png-Feb-02-2026-07-45-58-2949-PM.png)

3. You will see a pop-up window that asks you to confirm whether you want to delete them from this event, OR from all events within your organization. Click your preferred option.
