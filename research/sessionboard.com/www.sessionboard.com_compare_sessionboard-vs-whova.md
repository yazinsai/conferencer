## Quick answer

Whova is an all-in-one event platform — registration, mobile app, agenda, and basic abstract submission for associations and conferences. Sessionboard displaces Whova on the program layer when multi-round review, speaker CRM, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy matter. Whova fits smaller meetings wanting one vendor; Sessionboard fits medical, scientific, and multi-track events where program depth beats app-first packaging.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Abstract committee chair

Whova abstract forms exist — multi-round review and chair workflow still happen outside the product.

### Speaker operations manager

Speaker portal is thin — you need materials, bios, and agenda truth from acceptance through show day.

### VP Marketing

App engagement metrics without registration attribution — Advocacy needs tracked faculty shares.

### Scientific program director

500+ abstracts and 50+ reviewers — you need committee ops, not app-first abstract intake.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where Whova stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## Whova — where it ends

- **All-in-one packaging**
Registration, mobile app, and agenda in one contract for mid-market associations.

- **Attendee mobile app**
Branded app, networking, and agenda for smaller conferences.

- **Faster time-to-app**
App-first buyers who prioritize launch speed over review depth.


### The narrow case

- Under 200 abstracts, one review pass, and mobile app is the primary buying criteria
- Single-vendor mandate with no post-meeting content or advocacy requirements

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Multi-round abstract and committee ops**
Chair workflows, COI capture, and rubrics — not lightweight submission forms only.

- **Speaker CRM year over year**
Faculty history persists across annual cycles — not reset when the next Whova event opens.

- **Content marketing and Advocacy**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Create, and tracked peer shares on the session graph.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Medical or scientific congress with formal peer review
- Speaker CRM and materials pipeline beyond basic abstract intake
- Post-event transcription, clips, and Attendee Hub required
- Faculty and sponsor shares need registration attribution

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.
