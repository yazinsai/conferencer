## Quick answer

Grow your event by turning speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees into tracked promoters — not a one-size please-share blast. Sessionboard Advocacy fires personalized share pages and attributed links from program milestones (acceptance, agenda publish, early-bird, exhibitor activation, post-session). The Advocacy Agent auto-nudges each advocate type; Dispatch extends the same graph to internal teams. Snöball, Gleanin, and InGo excel at campaign UX when advocate rosters live outside the program graph.

## Growing the event?

Match your team to how Sessionboard turns speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees into attributed registration — not vanity impressions.

### Event marketing lead

Registration plateaus after launch — you need every advocate type sharing tracked links, not one generic event banner.

### Sponsorship / exhibitor sales

Packages promise amplification — deliver per-partner share hubs and registration attribution, not deck screenshots.

### Speaker operations

Accepted speakers intend to promote — then hit a blank page. Share packs should fire from acceptance on the program graph.

### Community / attendee marketing

Past attendees and members will refer colleagues — you need segmented advocate journeys, not a mass BCC.

## Evaluation checklist

- Can speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees each get personalized share assets — not one generic event graphic?
- Do tracked links attribute registrations (and optionally views) back to each advocate and channel?
- Does advocacy start at the right milestone — speaker acceptance, sponsor package live, exhibitor portal open, attendee early-bird?
- Can post-session clips and quote cards flow from transcription to the same advocate page?
- Does advocate CRM carry speakers, sponsors, exhibitors, and top referrers forward event over event?
- Can membership, sales, and GTM teams push approved content via Dispatch on the same graph?
- Do milestone prompts fire automatically — or does someone chase advocates by email?
- Can an Advocacy Agent personalize nudges and re-activate quiet advocates without manual follow-up?

## Frequently asked questions

What is speaker advocacy for events?▼

Equipping speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees with ready-to-share assets and tracked links so they promote your event to audiences that already trust them — designed as a milestone journey that grows registration, not a one-off please-share blast.

Why do willing advocates still not promote?▼

Usually friction, not motivation. Give each advocate type a sixty-second path: pre-filled posts, personal graphics, and one hub at the right milestone — acceptance, agenda publish, exhibitor activation, early-bird, or post-session clips.

What is Sessionboard Advocacy?▼

Program-native event marketing for speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees — personalized share pages and tracked links fired from call for papers, sponsor inventory, and CRM state, not a CSV import.

What is the Advocacy Agent?▼

Sessionboard's Advocacy Agent personalizes and auto-nudges advocates at program milestones — acceptance, agenda publish, early-bird, exhibitor go-live, post-session — with pre-filled assets and tracked links. Advocates stay activated; your team stops relying on manual follow-up emails.

What is Dispatch?▼

Internal team distribution for membership, sales, GTM, and marketing — same attribution layer as external Advocacy when program content is ready to push and grow registration.

How does Advocacy differ from bolt-on P2P tools?▼

Advocacy reads acceptance, session, sponsor, and transcript state on the program graph. Specialist tools excel at referral hub UX when roster data lives in registration — many teams use both to grow the event.

Can Sessionboard coexist with Snöball or Gleanin?▼

Yes — program truth and advocate CRM on Sessionboard; campaign UX on the specialist tool when that split fits your team.

What should we measure?▼

Share → click → registration per advocate and channel, rolled up event over event — the metrics that prove you grew the event, not impressions alone.
