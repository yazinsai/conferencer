## Two teams. One platform that grows your Informa show.

Sessionboard sits beside your Informa registration contract. Event and program teams own the authoritative session graph. Marketing and sponsorship teams grow registration, sponsor ROI, and reusable content from that same data — no CSV re-key, no disconnected advocacy campaigns.

### Event & program teams

Run the show pipeline on one graph — then clone what works across Informa Markets and Informa Connect franchises.

- CFP through committee review and speaker portal — chairs self-serve review rounds without configuration tickets.
- Speaker CRM persists faculty, moderators, and materials across annual cycles and sister Informa brands.
- Agenda and session truth from acceptance through show day — stop chasing bios and slides in email threads.
- Med/sci and multi-track workflows — COI, credit rules, and review rounds on the live session record.
- Clone committee templates to the next Informa franchise when your portfolio standardizes.

### Marketing & sponsorship teams

Grow registration, sponsor inventory, and content reuse — with attribution tied to accepted sessions, not vanity share counts.

- Advocacy fires at speaker acceptance — personalized share pages and tracked registration links before peak registration.
- Advocacy Agent auto-nudges faculty, exhibitors, and sponsors at milestones — grow attributed demand without manual chase emails.
- Package exhibitor and faculty amplification as measurable inventory — per-advocate and per-channel ROI for renewals.
- Dispatch pushes approved clips, agendas, and registration links to internal GTM, membership, and sales teams.
- Event-over-event reporting — know which SMEs and channels converted last year; compound your marketing footprint across the portfolio.
