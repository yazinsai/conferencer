## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from EventsAir.

- Attendee registers on EventsAir — registration record stays the system of record.
- Accepted speakers and sessions live in Sessionboard — program team works without re-keying exports.
- Agenda publish and speaker materials move via Sessionboard API, import, or export — not a single shipped connector.
- Live transcription and Attendee Hub deliver content from Sessionboard session records.

**API and import paths:** No formal EventsAir connector exists on either platform today. Most teams move program data through Sessionboard's API and import/export paths. If your event justifies deeper automation, we can scope options together during onboarding.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep EventsAir only when

- EventsAir registration contract is multi-year and non-negotiable
- APAC association standardized on EventsAir ops team

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Abstract committee pain is the bottleneck — not registration UX
- Need transcription, clips, and advocacy tied to program sessions
- Speaker identity must persist across years on one CRM

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside EventsAir.

Does Sessionboard replace EventsAir?▼

Usually not at first. Registration and event operations often stay on EventsAir while Sessionboard runs abstract review, speaker readiness, and content capture beside it.

How do Sessionboard and EventsAir connect?▼

There is no shipped connector between the two products today. Teams typically use Sessionboard's API and import/export for sessions, speakers, and agenda data. We can discuss tighter sync when you are ready.

Who owns registration data?▼

EventsAir remains the system of record for tickets and attendee registration. Sessionboard owns sessions, speakers, and program content.

Can we move off EventsAir over time?▼

Some organizations start with Sessionboard for program and speaker work while registration stays put, then revisit the broader stack when contracts and priorities allow. There is no one-size-fits-all path — we map it to your event.

What about abstract review?▼

Sessionboard runs CFP and committee review when EventsAir is your registration home — so accepted work does not live in email and spreadsheets.
