## Quick answer

Sched is conference agenda and scheduling software — session pages, speaker lists, and attendee-facing schedules for community and association events. Sessionboard displaces Sched on the program layer when multi-round abstract review, chair workflow, speaker materials, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy with attribution matter. Sched fits simpler agendas; Sessionboard fits medical, scientific, and multi-track programs with hundreds of submissions.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Abstract committee chair

Sched shows the agenda — review rounds and conflict rules still live outside the product.

### Speaker operations manager

Speaker portal is thin — you need materials, bios, and agenda truth from acceptance through show day.

### VP Marketing

Schedule views without registration attribution — Advocacy needs tracked faculty shares.

### Scientific program director

Hundreds of abstracts — you need committee ops, not agenda publishing alone.

## Sched — where it ends

- **Fast agenda publishing**
Quick session pages and schedules for smaller community events.

- **Attendee schedule UX**
Personal agendas and session discovery for mid-market conferences.

- **Lower complexity entry**
Fits teams prioritizing schedule visibility over review depth.


### The narrow case

- Community conference with simple CFP and agenda-first priorities
- Program team under fifty sessions with minimal review rounds

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Multi-round abstract review**
Chairs, rubrics, COI, and reviewer assignments on the program graph.

- **Speaker CRM year over year**
Faculty records persist across events — not re-imported each conference cycle.

- **Content and advocacy**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, clips, and Advocacy with registration attribution.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Multi-round abstract review with dozens of volunteer reviewers
- Speaker materials, AV, and run-of-show managed from acceptance
- Marketing needs attributed faculty promotion from session state
- Med/sci or association programs requiring COI and audit trails

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.
