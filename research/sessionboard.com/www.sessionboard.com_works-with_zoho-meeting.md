Sessionboard is the webinar program layer: request forms, SME Explorer across your speaker CRM, recording and transcript ingest, clips, Advocacy to hosts and attendees, Dispatch to internal teams, and efficacy back to CRM — via API and import paths beside your runtime.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Head of webinar marketing

Teams request webinars faster than you can find SMEs — intake forms and SME Explorer should rank experts from your full speaker CRM, not Slack threads.

### Content marketing

### Membership or community lead

### GTM and sales enablement

Field teams miss webinar moments — Dispatch delivers tracked clip packs; Advocacy lets hosts and panelists share with attribution.

## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Zoho Meeting.

- A team submits a webinar request — or marketing schedules one already on the runtime.
- SME Explorer surfaces ranked experts from your speaker CRM across annual meetings and past webinars.
- Live delivery stays on Zoho Meeting; Recordings and presenter lists reach Sessionboard via connector, API, or export from Zoho Meeting.
- Transcription and Create produce clips; Advocacy activates for external audiences; Dispatch sends internal packs.
- Shares, clicks, and influenced registrations roll up to CRM so program efficacy is visible.

**API and import paths:** Loose integration — Zoho Meeting for live delivery; Sessionboard for content graph, transcription, and SME history via API and export.

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside Zoho Meeting.

Does Sessionboard replace Zoho Meeting?▼

Usually not for live delivery. Zoho Meeting stays the runtime while Sessionboard runs webinar requests, SME discovery, recording ingest, clips, Advocacy, Dispatch, and measurement.

How do teams find SMEs for the next webinar?▼

Webinar request forms and SME Explorer search your org-wide speaker CRM — conference faculty, past webinar hosts, and panelists ranked by topic and engagement.

How do Sessionboard and Zoho Meeting connect?▼

There is no shipped connector between the two products today. Recordings and presenter data typically move via Sessionboard API and import/export. Tighter sync is scoped when your program justifies it.

What is the difference between Advocacy and Dispatch?▼

Advocacy activates hosts, speakers, and attendees with tracked external shares. Dispatch pushes clip packs and talking points to internal membership, GTM, and sales teams.

Can we measure program efficacy?▼

Sessionboard tracks shares, clicks, and influenced registrations from Advocacy and Dispatch recipients — efficacy data flows back to your CRM alongside webinar completion metrics.
