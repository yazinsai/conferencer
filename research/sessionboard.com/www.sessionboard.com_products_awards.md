What is Sessionboard Awards?

Sessionboard Awards is an integrated awards management platform designed for associations, conference organizers, and event teams. Unlike standalone awards software, it unifies recognition programs with session management, speaker data, and event content—allowing you to run fair, scalable awards programs while building long-term institutional knowledge.

How is Sessionboard Awards different from other award management software?

Traditional awards platforms operate in isolation from your event content and session management systems. Sessionboard Awards integrates directly with your speakers, sessions, and programming workflows—meaning award winners automatically enrich speaker profiles, finalists inform future agendas, and recognition becomes searchable content intelligence rather than a one-time list of winners.

Can we use Sessionboard Awards if our awards and education teams are separate?

Yes. Sessionboard Awards is designed to support organizations in which awards and education are managed by separate teams. Each team maintains ownership of its processes while gaining access to shared, reliable data. This eliminates duplicate work, reduces manual reconciliation, and creates better outcomes for both teams—all within a unified platform.

What features are included in Sessionboard Awards?

● Customizable submission forms and nomination workflows

● Configurable review and scoring systems with multiple evaluation criteria

● Automated communications for nominees, finalists, and winners

● Integration with speaker profiles and session data

● Real-time reporting and analytics dashboards

● Flexible award categories and program structures

● Sponsor and partner recognition capabilities

How does community voting work in Sessionboard Awards?

Community voting turns your audience into active participants in the awards process. Enable voting for specific categories, set custom voting windows, and decide whether votes influence final winners or create separate "People's Choice" awards. Voters browse nominees, cast votes, and share their participation—naturally expanding your brand's reach. Real-time dashboards track engagement and help you identify your most passionate community members. It's more than polling—it's a strategic tool for deeper audience investment and organic brand growth.

Can Sessionboard Awards help reduce bias in our awards program?

Yes. Sessionboard Awards includes multiple features designed to promote fairness: AI-assisted evaluation that highlights outlier scores and inconsistencies, configurable blind review options, normalized scoring across panels, and clear audit trails. These tools help review committees make more objective, defensible decisions while maintaining transparency throughout the process.

Does Sessionboard Awards integrate with our existing event technology stack?

Yes. Sessionboard is built to integrate seamlessly with modern event technology ecosystems, including registration platforms, event apps, marketing automation tools, and CRM systems. Our team works with you to ensure Awards fits naturally into your existing workflows and technical infrastructure.

Can we manage multiple award programs in Sessionboard?

Yes. Sessionboard Awards supports multiple award programs, categories, and workflows within a single platform. Whether you run one signature awards program or manage several recognition initiatives throughout the year, you can configure each with unique submission requirements, review processes, scoring criteria, and timelines.

What types of organizations use Sessionboard Awards?

Sessionboard Awards is designed for associations, conference organizers, event marketing teams, and any organization that manages both awards programs and event content. Our customers include professional societies, industry associations, corporate event teams, and nonprofit organizations running recognition programs alongside educational conferences and events.

Does Sessionboard Awards support sponsor recognition and branded awards?

Yes. Sessionboard Awards include flexible sponsor and partner recognition features. You can create sponsored award categories, acknowledge sponsors throughout the awards process, and provide sponsors with visibility into relevant submissions and winners—all while maintaining program integrity and confidentiality where needed.

How does Sessionboard Awards help with year-round content strategy?

By connecting awards to speakers, sessions, and topics, Sessionboard transforms recognition into actionable content intelligence. You can quickly identify award winners for speaking opportunities, understand which topics resonate with your audience, track expertise across your community, and make data-informed programming decisions based on recognition patterns over time.

What kind of reporting and analytics does Sessionboard Awards provide?

Sessionboard Awards includes comprehensive reporting on submissions, review progress, scoring distributions, category performance, and program outcomes. You can generate reports for internal stakeholders, export data for analysis, and access real-time dashboards that track program health throughout the awards cycle.

Is Sessionboard Awards secure and compliant?

Yes. Sessionboard is built with enterprise-grade security, including data encryption, role-based access controls, and compliance with industry standards. Sensitive submission materials, reviewer feedback, and personal information are protected throughout the awards process, with full audit trails for accountability and transparency.

What support is available for Sessionboard Awards customers?

Sessionboard customers receive dedicated support throughout implementation and ongoing program management. This includes onboarding and training, configuration assistance, best practice guidance, technical support, and access to our customer success team. We're committed to helping you run successful, impactful awards programs.

How much does Sessionboard Awards cost?

Sessionboard Awards pricing is based on your program needs, submission volume, and platform configuration. Contact our team for a personalized quote and demo. We'll work with you to understand your requirements and design a solution that fits your organization and budget.

How do I get started with Sessionboard Awards?

Getting started is easy. Schedule a demo to see Sessionboard Awards in action, discuss your program requirements with our team, and learn how unified awards and session management can strengthen your event content strategy. Visit our Awards page or contact us directly to begin the conversation.
