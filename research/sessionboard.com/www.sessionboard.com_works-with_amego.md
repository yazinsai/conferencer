## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep Amego mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## Amego — their lane

- **Branded mobile apps**
Enterprise iOS/Android apps, Sidekick AI, and attendee engagement at scale.

- **Exhibitor lead capture**
Booth scanning and CRM sync for sponsor ROI inside the app.

- **App delivery and support**
Amego owns deployment, push notifications, and attendee UX.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Authoritative program graph**
Agenda, speakers, rooms, and changes from program team — before app publish.

- **Speaker CRM year over year**
Faculty history across annual cycles — not reset each Amego event.

- **Content and Advocacy**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, clips, and tracked peer shares beside the app.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Amego.

- Program team publishes agenda in Sessionboard — sessions, speakers, rooms, times.
- Export or integration feeds Amego app configuration — Amego owns mobile shell.
- Last-minute session changes originate in Sessionboard — reduce stale schedule risk.
- Optional deep links from Amego app to Sessionboard Attendee Hub for replays and clips.

**Integration status:** Amego owns the mobile app; Sessionboard supplies the authoritative program graph via API or export patterns your teams implement.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep Amego only when

- Enterprise Amego mobile app already contracted for the portfolio
- Large trade show or association event with dedicated app team

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Program data still re-keyed from spreadsheets into Amego
- Need formal abstract review and speaker portal before app build
- Post-event content library beyond in-app agenda browsing
