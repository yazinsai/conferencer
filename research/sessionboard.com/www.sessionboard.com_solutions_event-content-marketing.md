## Why post-event content stalls

Marketing teams inherit AV folders and slide decks — disconnected from session titles, speakers, and learning objectives already in the program system.

- Post-event marketing starts with manual exports — session metadata, bios, and abstracts re-entered into Canva, CMS, and email tools.
- Transcripts and clips live in vendor folders without ties to registration attribution or speaker identity across years.
- Horizontal social tools cannot generate share copy from what was actually said on stage — generic posts underperform faculty promotion.
- Webinar and in-person sessions treated as separate content projects when attendees expect one program experience.

## How the content pipeline works

One session record from acceptance through capture, publish, and amplification — beside your registration or webinar platform.

- Session scheduled and speaker confirmed → program graph holds title, track, learning objectives, and bio before doors open.
- Live or uploaded capture → Live Transcribe and Media Library attach transcripts, chapters, and quote libraries to the session record.
- Content ready for attendees → Create templates and Attendee Hub publish recaps, takeaways, and searchable session pages without a CMS re-build.
- Marketing window opens → Advocacy assigns tracked share links and AI copy variants to speakers and sponsors; the Advocacy Agent auto-nudges at milestones; Dispatch pushes recipes to internal teams.
- Year-round learning → packaged transcripts, clips, and Attendee Hub objects sync to Path, TopClass, CloudCME, EthosCE, and other LMS platforms — see works-with guides.
- Measurement closes the loop → registration attribution on Advocacy links plus engagement on Attendee Hub — not impressions alone.

## Frequently asked questions

Content marketing on the program graph — not a separate DAM import.

Can session content flow to our LMS?▼

Yes — Sessionboard Content Marketing packages transcripts, clips, and Attendee Hub learning objects for Path, TopClass, CloudCME, Oasis, EthosCE, Elevate, and other platforms. Credit reporting stays on your LMS where contracted.

Does Sessionboard replace our marketing automation platform?▼

No. Sessionboard supplies session-grounded content and tracked Advocacy links; you keep HubSpot, Marketo, or Pardot for nurture. Attendee Hub can complement or feed your public site.

How does this work with our registration platform?▼

Registration stays on Cvent, RainFocus, Eventbrite, or your AMS. Sessionboard owns program and content — sync paths are documented on works-with pages for your stack.

What about webinar-only events?▼

The same Media Library, Create, and Attendee Hub stack applies when sessions live in Zoom, ON24, Goldcast, or other runtimes — Sessionboard holds the program layer beside the webinar tool.

Do we need a separate video editor?▼

Create produces clip variants and quote cards from transcripts in Media Library. Heavy brand post-production can still export assets — but most teams stop re-keying session metadata.

How is this different from a transcription add-on?▼

Transcription is one step. Sessionboard connects capture to speaker identity, Attendee Hub, Advocacy tracked links, and multi-year faculty history — not a one-off transcript file.

Can sponsors get content packages?▼

Yes — sponsor Advocacy campaigns tie booth inventory to tracked links and clip templates from sessions they support.

What do we measure?▼

Attendee Hub engagement, clip views, Advocacy click-through, and attributed registrations — benchmarked per session and per advocate.
