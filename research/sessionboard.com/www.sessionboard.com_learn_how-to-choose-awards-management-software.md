## Quick answer

Awards management software spans standalone judging platforms (AwardForce, Evessio), horizontal submission tools (Submittable, OpenWater), and program-native suites that can run awards-only or connect awards to conference speaker CRM. Choose standalone tools for narrow judging-season scope; choose a program graph when you need SSO for members, submission dedupe and merge, robust multi-round judging, and optional tie-in to faculty, advocacy, and events.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Program manager

Chairs and reviewers still coordinate in email — you need committee ops on the program graph, not another submission form.

### Speaker operations

Speaker data resets every submission cycle — you need one faculty record from acceptance through show day.

### Marketing director

Social shares without registration attribution — Advocacy tracks peer links; Dispatch covers internal team distribution.

### Content marketing

Transcripts and clips live in a separate tool — you need capture, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record.

## Vendor landscape

No single winner — match the tool to your trigger, data ownership, and team workflow.

| Vendor | Best when | Tradeoffs | Pricing note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AwardForce | Awards or grants are the primary buy; judging UX and security certifications matter most. | Conference speaker CRM and post-meeting content typically live elsewhere. | Per-program licensing; strong for awards-only associations. |
| Evessio | UK/EU association awards with focused judging seasons and awards-only scope. | Limited tie to call for papers, speaker portal, and content marketing. | Association awards specialist positioning. |
| OpenWater | Multiple form-based programs (awards, grants, abstracts) on one horizontal contract. | Post-acceptance speaker workflow and event-native content often still manual. | Breadth across program types; depth varies by module. |
| Submittable | Grants and fellowships across nonprofit use cases — not always event-linked. | Event program graph and faculty CRM not the core product. | Horizontal submission platform pricing. |
| Sessionboard | Robust awards for associations, trade shows, and corporate — awards-only or awards plus conference. SSO for existing members, submission dedupe and merge, multi-round judging at scale. | Full program platform — strongest when you want identity and reporting to compound; lighter point tools exist for single-season judging with no CRM graph. | Licensing scoped to program volume; many customers run Awards without a conference. |

## Evaluation checklist

- Do you need SSO so only members or approved users can submit or judge?
- Will duplicate nominations or repeat entrants require dedupe and record merge?
- Do winners routinely speak at or promote your annual conference (if you run one)?
- Should judges and abstract reviewers share identity and history across years?
- Is registration attribution required for winner and faculty promotion?
- Does the board require portfolio reporting across multiple awards programs?

## Frequently asked questions

Awards-only vs conference plus awards?▼

Both are valid. AwardForce and Evessio excel at awards-only judging UX. Sessionboard runs robust awards-only programs — SSO for members, dedupe and merge, multi-round judging — and adds conference, speaker CRM, and advocacy when you need them.

OpenWater vs dedicated awards tools?▼

OpenWater wins breadth across form types; dedicated awards tools win judging UX; program graphs win when faculty identity must persist across abstract, awards, and advocacy workflows.

Can we run two systems temporarily?▼

Yes — common during migration; minimize duplicate entry with clear system-of-record rules for faculty data.

What is the Sessionboard wedge?▼

Awards-only when you need member SSO, dedupe, and enterprise judging — or start Awards when a conference contract is locked elsewhere; expand to call for papers on the same speaker CRM when ready.

Security and compliance?▼

Compare ISO/SOC posture for your industry — AwardForce publishes extensive certifications; validate your society requirements against each vendor trust center.
