## Why teams choose Sessionboard

## Snapsight — where it ends

- **Translation breadth at scale**
Real-time translation across 75+ languages, deployed at large trade shows and congresses, and that is the deployment's scope.

- **AV-line simplicity**
Capture starts from a mixer feed or a meeting bot, with drag-and-drop for old recordings. Easy in, and everything lands in their portal.

- **Trade-show references**
Deployments at IMEX, ICCA, and UFI events for real-time takeaway feeds on show floors.


### The narrow case

- Real-time translation across dozens of languages is the entire requirement and the program runs nowhere in particular
- A trade-show floor wants a takeaway ticker for co-located shows with no speaker or session operations behind it

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **The program graph under the content**
Transcripts, summaries, and clips attach to sessions that already carry abstract text, speakers, learning objectives, and credits from call for papers through agenda publish. Snapsight starts from raw audio and guesses.

- **Attendee Hub on your domain**
Branded attendee microsite with replays, transcripts, session summaries, event and daily reports, and AI chat grounded in your event content. Not a vendor portal your attendees leave your brand to visit.

- **Live transcription and Media Library**
Live Transcribe capture, batch transcription with diarization, AI summaries, quote extraction, and a searchable multi-year library on the session graph.

- **Studio, Create, and Remix**
AI clip suggestions, branded clip render, quote cards, posts, and emails generated from transcripts that know the session's own metadata.

- **Distribution, not just generation**
Advocacy gives speakers and attendees tracked share pages fired from program milestones, and Dispatch routes content to internal teams. Generated content that nobody distributes is a folder.

- **Speaker CRM and the program itself**
The people on stage live in a multi-year speaker CRM beside the call for papers, review, and agenda. A content vendor renews a subscription; a program platform compounds.

- **Public API write-back**
write:transcriptions, write:media, and session recordings keep one program record if any external capture runs. See apidocs.sessionboard.com.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Call for papers, review, or speaker management already runs on Sessionboard, so capture should start from that graph
- Session summaries, event reports, and clips should carry your brand on your domain, not a vendor portal
- Attendees should ask questions of event content inside your own Attendee Hub
- The speakers who drew the audience should distribute the content through tracked Advocacy links
- You want a multi-year content library tied to a speaker CRM, not a per-event subscription that resets
- Medical and scientific societies need capture connected to credits, disclosures, and learning objectives
- Budget review favors one program-plus-content platform over program SaaS plus a content subscription

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against Snapsight for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Does Sessionboard replace Snapsight?▼

Yes, for session capture, AI summaries, clips, attendee-facing content, and post-event reuse. Sessionboard ships those natively on the session record, adds Advocacy distribution and a speaker CRM, and runs the program itself, which a capture vendor cannot.

Snapsight generates 20+ content types from an audio feed. What does Sessionboard generate?▼

Transcripts, AI summaries, session and event reports, daily debriefs, clips, quote cards, social posts, and emails through Media Library, Studio, and Create. The difference is context: Sessionboard's outputs start from a session that knows its own abstract, speakers, and objectives, so the content needs less correction and carries the right names.

What about real-time summaries during the event?▼

Live Transcribe captures sessions as they happen, and summaries land in Attendee Hub where attendees already see the agenda, replays, and speakers. There is no separate portal to drive traffic to.

Does Sessionboard support translation?▼

Transcription and AI outputs support the languages most programs need. If a show floor requires simultaneous translation across dozens of languages, run that feed alongside and write the audio back to the session record through the public API.

We already use Snapsight. What does switching look like?▼

Bring the recordings: Media Library ingests audio and video in batch with transcription and diarization, so past events join the same searchable library as your next one. Program data migrates through the standard onboarding path.

How does pricing compare?▼

Snapsight prices as a per-event or annual content subscription on top of whatever runs your program. Sessionboard prices the program platform with content capture included. Request a demo for a scoped quote.
