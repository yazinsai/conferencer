## Quick answer

Evessio is awards and judging software for associations and membership organizations. Sessionboard Awards connects submissions and judging to conference call for papers, speaker portal, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy — one speaker CRM for nominees, judges, winners, and faculty across annual cycles. Evessio fits awards-only scope; Sessionboard fits when winners become session speakers and judges return next season.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Awards program manager

Evessio completes judging — winners re-keyed into conference speaker lists; you need one faculty record.

### Conference program director

Award recipients should present at the meeting — acceptance should open speaker portal tasks automatically.

### Marketing director

Winner gallery without tracked shares — Advocacy should fire from acceptance on the program graph.

### Portfolio governance lead

Board wants awards metrics across sister events — you need CRM rollups, not per-program exports.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where Evessio stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## Evessio — where it ends

- **Association awards focus**
Workflows tuned for membership awards, categories, and judging seasons.

- **Judging and winner UX**
Applicant and judge portals for awards-centric programs.

- **Awards-only procurement**
Fits when conference program is out of scope for three-plus years.


### The narrow case

- Awards-only program with no annual meeting speaker or content requirements
- Judging season is the sole digital workflow for the foreseeable contract term

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Speaker CRM across awards and conference**
Nominees, judges, winners, and session faculty share one record — persists event over event.

- **Abstract plus awards on one graph**
Call for papers, committee review, and awards judging share chair tools and faculty history.

- **Content and advocacy after winners**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, clips, and tracked peer shares on winner and ceremony sessions.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Winners routinely speak at or promote the annual conference
- Same volunteers serve as abstract reviewers and awards judges
- Marketing needs attributed winner and faculty shares — not a static gallery
- Portfolio reporting requires SME and advocate history across multiple events

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against Evessio for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Does Sessionboard replace Evessio?▼

When awards and conference should share speaker CRM — yes. Awards-only associations may stay on Evessio until conference program needs appear.

Can judges from last year carry forward?▼

Yes — judge and reviewer records persist on speaker CRM; assignments clone across cycles.

What about abstract review?▼

Same platform — multi-round CFP review shares workflows and faculty identity with Awards.

Can we start with awards only?▼

Yes — common entry when registration or abstract contracts are locked on another vendor.

How does winner promotion work?▼

Advocacy issues tracked share assets from acceptance — registration attribution on the same graph as sessions.
