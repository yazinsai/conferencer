## EthosCE — their lane

- **Learning platform delivery**
Courses, activities, learner progress, and catalog UX in EthosCE.

- **CE/CME credit reporting**
CME and MOC reporting on EthosCE — competitive coexistence, not LMS replacement.

- **Year-round learner experience**
On-demand library, certifications, and compliance reporting on EthosCE.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Session and webinar capture**
Live Transcribe, batch transcription, and Media Library on the program graph.

- **Content packaging for LMS**
Transcripts, clips, and Attendee Hub objects from Sessionboard → EthosCE activities — without re-importing from AV folders.

- **Program-native metadata**
Titles, speakers, learning objectives, and abstracts already in Sessionboard — not re-entered in EthosCE.

- **Advocacy and Attendee Hub**
Attendee-facing recaps plus peer marketing before content lands in EthosCE.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from EthosCE.

- Session or webinar runs — capture lands in Sessionboard Media Library with program metadata.
- Create produces clips, takeaways, and Attendee Hub pages on the session record.
- Packaged learning objects export to EthosCE courses or activities via API or partner sync.
- Learners consume on-demand content in EthosCE; credit reporting stays on the LMS where configured.
- Next event cycle reuses the same faculty graph — no reset between submission years.

**API and import paths:** Coexistence with Cadmium learning — Sessionboard Content Marketing syncs session artifacts into EthosCE while abstract and speaker ops stay on Sessionboard. Credit reporting remains on EthosCE where contracted.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep EthosCE only when

- EthosCE contract is multi-year and CE catalog is growing
- Conference and webinar content should extend year-round learning
- Manual LMS upload after each meeting is the current pain

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- High session volume with transcription and clip requirements
- Faculty and metadata should not be re-keyed into EthosCE
- Content Marketing and webinar program run on Sessionboard today
- Integrate with your AMS works-with page for member-validated CFP

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside EthosCE.

Does Sessionboard replace EthosCE?▼

No. Sessionboard integrates with EthosCE — we capture and package content from events and webinars; EthosCE delivers learning, progress, and credit reporting.

What content syncs from Sessionboard to EthosCE?▼

Transcripts, clips, and Attendee Hub objects from Sessionboard → EthosCE activities — without re-importing from AV folders

Who owns CE and CME credit?▼

CME and MOC reporting on EthosCE — competitive coexistence, not LMS replacement

Do we need Sessionboard abstract management too?▼

Many associations run full program ops on Sessionboard — CFP through capture — then feed EthosCE. Abstract-only on another tool still works; capture quality improves on one graph.

How does this relate to Cadmium?▼

Sessionboard competes with Cadmium on program ops, not on LMS delivery. See works-with pages for EthosCE and Elevate when Cadmium learning is in the stack.

Is the connector native?▼
