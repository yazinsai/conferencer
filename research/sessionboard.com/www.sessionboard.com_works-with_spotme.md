## SpotMe — their lane

- **Mobile event experiences**
Branded apps, networking, polls, and real-time attendee engagement.

- **CRM and martech sync**
Bidirectional integration with Salesforce, HubSpot, and life-sciences stacks.

- **Enterprise event delivery**
SpotMe owns attendee UX and managed services for global brands.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Program and speaker graph**
Sessions, faculty, materials, and agenda changes as system of record.

- **Content capture and Attendee Hub**
Transcription, clips, and on-demand library beside SpotMe live experiences.

- **Advocacy with attribution**
Tracked HCP and faculty shares from acceptance — complements app engagement data.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from SpotMe.

- Program published in Sessionboard — sessions, speakers, and materials collected pre-event.
- Agenda and speaker data export or sync into SpotMe event configuration.
- Live engagement captured in SpotMe flows to CRM — program identity anchored in Sessionboard.
- Post-event replays and clips in Attendee Hub — link from SpotMe app where configured.

**Integration status:** SpotMe owns mobile experiences and CRM engagement; Sessionboard supplies program depth via export or integration.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep SpotMe only when

- Enterprise SpotMe mobile mandate for field medical or brand events
- CRM team requires engagement data alongside formal speaker program ops

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Abstract or speaker workflow still in email and spreadsheets upstream of SpotMe
- Post-event content reuse required beyond event app lifespan
