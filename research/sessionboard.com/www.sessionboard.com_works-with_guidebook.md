## Quick answer

Guidebook is a mobile event app platform with drag-and-drop CMS for associations, universities, and mid-market conferences. Sessionboard holds call for papers, committee review, speaker portal, and agenda truth — reducing spreadsheet exports into Guidebook builds when program teams change sessions or speakers.

Guidebook agenda built from exports — Sessionboard should publish authoritative session data.

Speaker changes after Guidebook publish — originate updates in Sessionboard first.

App is separate from abstract workflow — unify faculty CRM before attendee-facing publish.

## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep Guidebook mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## Guidebook — their lane

- **Event app CMS**
Drag-and-drop mobile app and web agenda builder.

- **Attendee mobile UX**
Guidebook owns app store deployment and in-app engagement.

- **Mid-market time-to-app**
Fast launches for associations and university events.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Abstract and speaker program**
CFP, review, portal, and program build before app publish.

- **Speaker CRM**
Faculty records persist across annual Guidebook events.

- **Content and Advocacy**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, and tracked shares beside the app.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Guidebook.

- Sessionboard publishes program — sessions, speakers, rooms, times.
- Export or integration updates Guidebook agenda — Guidebook owns app shell.
- Last-minute changes managed in Sessionboard — republish to reduce stale app data.
- Optional links to Sessionboard-hosted replays from Guidebook sessions.

**Integration status:** Guidebook owns the app; Sessionboard supplies program data via export or integration your team configures.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep Guidebook only when

- Guidebook app already chosen for association or campus events
- Formal abstract review happens outside Guidebook today

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Program team still maintains master agenda in Excel parallel to Guidebook
- Need multi-round review, speaker portal, or post-event content library
