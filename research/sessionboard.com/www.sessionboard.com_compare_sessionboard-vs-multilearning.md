## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Posters on the program graph**
Poster tracks in call for papers, shared multi-round review with platform abstracts, and program numbers that never go stale — no export/re-import cycle.

- **One speaker portal for every author**
Poster authors and platform faculty share one identity — bios, uploads, consent, and communications in the portal they already use.

- **Branded gallery on your program**
A searchable poster hall on your event's look with instant search, bookmarks, author share kits, and QR signage links.

- **Compliance and analytics built in**
Versioned consent, disclosures, embargo with teaser mode, withdrawn abstracts excluded automatically, and per-poster engagement reporting.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Posters share a call for papers with platform sessions and need the same review rounds and committees
- One speaker portal should carry poster and platform authors — bios, materials, consent, communications
- The gallery must live on your brand with engagement analytics your committee can read
- Registration stays on Cvent, Cadmium, or RainFocus — the program layer should not fragment further

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against MULTILEARNING for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Is Sessionboard a full MULTILEARNING replacement?▼

For the poster program, yes — acceptance through published gallery with analytics. MULTILEARNING's broader LMS (accredited e-learning, content sales) is a different product category; societies running CME on it often move the poster program to Sessionboard while the LMS stays.

Does Sessionboard support audio or video on posters?▼

Posters render crisply in the browser with AI plain-language summaries and ask-the-author built in. Supplementary media lives on the same session record via the Media Library rather than a separate hosting silo.

How do posters get into the gallery?▼

Authors upload in the Sessionboard speaker portal — the same place they submit bios and headshots. Accepted abstracts already carry titles, authors, and program numbers, so there is no export to a second vendor.

What about oral poster sessions on the program?▼

Poster sessions are scheduled on the published program beside platform talks, with the same speaker records and session pages — not a parallel calendar in another system.

Can attendees access posters after the meeting?▼

Yes. The gallery persists year-round on your brand, and multi-event organizers keep a poster library across annual meetings.

How does pricing compare?▼

MULTILEARNING prices ePoster hosting as a per-meeting service alongside its LMS offerings. Sessionboard pricing follows meeting and submission volume — request a demo for a scoped quote.
