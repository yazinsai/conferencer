What is a speaker CRM, and how is it different from a general CRM?

A speaker Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system is a purpose-built database designed to manage relationships with conference speakers, panelists, and subject-matter experts across multiple events. Unlike a general CRM (such as Salesforce or HubSpot), a speaker CRM stores speaker-specific data — bios, session history, topic expertise, travel preferences, and past engagement — and makes that data actionable for event planning. Sessionboard's Speaker CRM lets you search your entire speaker network by topic, tag, or event history so you can find and activate the right voices in minutes, not hours.

How does Sessionboard's speaker database help teams manage speakers across multiple events?

Sessionboard maintains a centralized speaker database that persists across every event you run. Each speaker profile accumulates history — sessions delivered, ratings received, content submitted, and communication logs — so your team never has to start from scratch. Dynamic speaker lists update automatically as profiles change, and advanced tagging makes it easy to filter by expertise, industry, geography, or engagement status. Teams managing four or more events per year consistently report significant time savings by reusing speaker data rather than rebuilding it each cycle — Sessionboard customers manage events up to 4 times faster than with manual processes.

Can Sessionboard be used as a call-for-speakers platform?

Yes. Sessionboard combines a Speaker CRM with a full call-for-speakers workflow, so you can run your speaker submission process and manage ongoing relationships in one place. You can build custom submission forms, set deadlines, assign reviewers, and move accepted speakers directly into your speaker database — all without switching platforms. This makes Sessionboard a strong alternative to standalone call-for-speakers platforms that do not connect to your broader event program.

How is a speaker database different from managing speakers in a spreadsheet?

Spreadsheets break down the moment a speaker updates their bio, a session gets rescheduled, or a second event team needs access to the same data. A speaker database like Sessionboard keeps all speaker records live, searchable, and shared across your entire organization. Profiles update in real time, communication history is logged automatically, and role-based permissions ensure each team member sees only what they need. The result is a single source of truth that eliminates version conflicts, duplicate outreach, and lost context between events.

What integrations does Sessionboard support — and how does it connect to tools like Cvent, Bizzabo, and Swoogo?

Sessionboard connects to more than 15 event management platforms — including Cvent, Bizzabo, and Swoogo — so speaker data flows into and out of the tools your team already uses. Speaker information entered once in Sessionboard is available across your Call for Papers (CFP) process, agenda builder, content management system, and speaker portals without re-entry or copy-pasting. Data import is supported via smart auto-mapping, making it straightforward to migrate an existing speaker database into Sessionboard from any source.

What types of organizations benefit most from speaker management software?

Any organization that runs recurring events with speakers benefits from a dedicated speaker management platform. This includes professional associations, corporate marketing teams, medical conference organizers, B2B media companies, and trade associations. The more frequently you run events or the larger your speaker network, the greater the return. Sessionboard customers include organizations running four to 40+ events per year, with speaker rosters ranging from dozens to thousands.

Is Sessionboard's speaker CRM compliant with data privacy regulations?

Yes. Sessionboard is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and certified to SOC 2 Type II (System and Organization Controls) standards, meaning your speaker data is stored, processed, and protected in accordance with current industry standards for data security and privacy. This is especially important for organizations managing speaker data across international events or subject to regulatory requirements for handling personal data.

How does Sessionboard work for organizations managing a portfolio of multiple events per year?

Sessionboard is built for portfolio-scale operations. Rather than treating each event as a separate instance, the platform maintains a continuous speaker database, content record, and workflow history that spans your entire program. Speaker profiles built during one event carry forward automatically to the next, submission history is searchable across years and activations, and template-based configurations let you replicate a proven workflow across new events without starting from scratch. Organizations managing 10 or more events per year consistently report that Sessionboard reduces setup time for each new program — because most of the foundational work is already in place.

Can a Professional Conference Organizer use Sessionboard to manage events across multiple client organizations?

Yes. Sessionboard is used by Professional Conference Organizers (PCOs) managing events across multiple client programs simultaneously. Each event can be configured with its own branded speaker portal, independent submission forms, separate reviewer committees, and isolated content workflows — while the PCO's team retains centralized visibility across all programs from a single account. This means clean data separation between clients without the overhead of managing entirely separate platform instances for each program.
