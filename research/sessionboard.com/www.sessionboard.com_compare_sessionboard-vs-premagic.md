## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where Premagic stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## Premagic — where it ends

- **AI photo distribution**
Face-matched galleries delivered to every attendee after the event — Premagic's origin is event photography, and this piece is genuinely theirs. Sessionboard does not do photo UGC.

- **Attendee poster generation at scale**
Personalized "I'm attending" posters with referral links for every registrant, plus branded AI avatars for social buzz.

- **Large consumer-scale event references**
GITEX Global and other Middle East mega-events with six-figure attendance — strong for expo-style poster campaigns.


### The narrow case

- Photo-first expo or consumer-scale show where AI photo distribution and attendee posters are the whole program
- No speaker or session program to speak of — pure attendee-volume marketing

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Advocacy fired from program state**
Tracked links and AI share copy generated at acceptance; the Advocacy Agent nudges at agenda publish, early-bird, and post-session. Premagic imports advocates after the fact and chases them with generic weekly cadences.

- **Every advocate type, one reporting layer**
Speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, attendees, and internal teams (membership, sales, GTM via Dispatch) on one graph — not an attendee-poster tool with speaker cards added on.

- **Registration attribution, not vanity EMV**
Per-advocate, per-channel clicks and attributed registrations, rolled up event over event. Earned-media-value estimates don't renew sponsors; attributed registrations do.

- **Speaker CRM across years**
Faculty, presenters, and top advocates persist across annual cycles, so each show starts with a known promoter graph instead of a fresh CSV import.

- **Session content built in**
Quote cards from transcripts, AI clips, session pages, and post-event content from Media Library and Create — share assets that carry the actual program, not just a face on a template.

- **The program layer underneath**
CFP, review, speaker portal, and agenda live here. Advocacy is a feature of the system of record, not another vendor between your speakers and your registration data.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Speakers, abstracts, or sessions run on Sessionboard — advocacy should fire from that graph, not a re-imported list
- You need exhibitor and sponsor amplification packages with per-link ROI to defend and resell sponsorship inventory
- Marketing needs attributed registrations by advocate and channel, rolling up across events and years
- Internal teams — membership, sales, GTM — should push the same tracked content through Dispatch
- Speaker CRM should compound: this year's presenters are next year's promoter graph
- Conference or association budgets favor one program-plus-advocacy platform over poster SaaS plus program SaaS
- Post-event content (clips, quote cards, session reports) should come from your own transcript pipeline

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against Premagic for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Does Sessionboard replace Premagic?▼

For speaker, exhibitor, sponsor, and attendee advocacy with registration attribution — yes, and from the program graph you already run. The exception is photo UGC: AI-matched photo galleries are Premagic's specialty and Sessionboard does not offer them.

Premagic says speaker posters drive up to 40% more registrations. Can Sessionboard do that motion?▼

Yes, and earlier in the cycle. Share assets generate at acceptance with tracked links, the Advocacy Agent nudges at each milestone, and attribution ties posts to registrations by advocate and channel — the same flywheel without a second vendor holding your data.

What about "I'm speaking" cards for every speaker?▼

Native. Personalized share pages and AI share copy come from the session record, so the card carries the real session title, time, and abstract — and updates when the agenda changes instead of going stale.

How is attribution different from Premagic's referral links?▼

Both track clicks. Sessionboard ties them to registrations per advocate and per channel, then rolls performance up event over event on the speaker CRM, so you know which faculty to activate first next year.

Can exhibitors and sponsors use it?▼

Yes — booth and session campaigns with per-advocate landing pages and per-link ROI, built to be resold as amplification inventory in the sponsorship contract.

When should we pay for both?▼

When photo distribution is contractually core to your attendee experience this cycle. Run advocacy and the program on Sessionboard; keep Premagic for the galleries until the contract lapses.
