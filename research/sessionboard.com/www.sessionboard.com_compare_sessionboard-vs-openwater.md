## Quick answer

Sessionboard is awards management software for associations that also run a meeting: nominations, multi-round judging, winner promotion, and the same speaker CRM as the annual program. OpenWater is a strong forms-and-review engine for awards, grants, and scholarships in one contract; it fits when judging season is the whole job. Sessionboard fits when winners become faculty, judges return every cycle, and peer promotion should fire from the same record.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where OpenWater stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## OpenWater — where it ends

- **Multi-program forms in one contract**
Awards, grants, scholarships, fellowships, and abstracts can share one OpenWater subscription when breadth of application types beats depth on the meeting.

- **Association channel presence**
ASAE Strategic Partner seal, homepage logos (ASAE, APS, Toy Association, AAF, IET), and mega-menu integrations for iMIS, Salesforce, and Higher Logic.

- **Published review rubrics**
Multi-round scoring and judge assignment for application-style workflows when review is the only job anyone needs done.


### The narrow case

- Awards, grants, and scholarships are the primary buy and there is no annual-meeting speaker layer to run
- One generic forms contract across many small programs matters more than a shared contact graph with the conference

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Awards on the same CRM as the meeting**
Nominees, judges, winners, and faculty share one identity across cycles — winners do not re-key into a separate speaker tool.

- **Speaker graph through the meeting**
Bio, headshot, slides, AV, consent, and conflict of interest (COI) tracked per person across years — not reset each submission cycle.

- **Peer marketing from program events**
Advocacy — tracked peer shares when winners and sessions are ready. Dispatch — internal team distribution with registration attribution.

- **Live content stack on the session record**
Transcription, Media Library, AI clips, Attendee Hub, and Create — no second upload step after the program is set.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Association awards program that should share judges, nominees, and winners with the annual meeting
- Committee still chases speakers in email after OpenWater acceptance
- Medical or scientific society with 500+ abstracts, COI rules, and multi-round chair workflow
- Need transcription, captions, clips, and on-demand library on the program you already run
- Advocacy and Dispatch — peer promotion from winners, speakers, and sponsors with registration attribution

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against OpenWater for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Is Sessionboard an OpenWater alternative for awards?▼

Yes — for association awards that should live beside the annual meeting. OpenWater excels at intake and multi-round review across awards, grants, and scholarships. Sessionboard runs nominations, judging, and winner promotion on the same contact graph as speakers and faculty, then carries accepted work through the meeting.

How does OpenWater compare on association awards?▼

OpenWater is the category incumbent associations know — ASAE partner, AMS bridges, strong judging UX. Sessionboard is the fit when winners become faculty, judges return every cycle, and marketing needs tracked shares from the same record — not a CSV export.

Can we keep OpenWater for grants and use Sessionboard for awards and the meeting?▼

Yes. Many associations run grants on a forms tool and consolidate awards plus the annual program on Sessionboard so member identity is not split three ways.

How does OpenWater compare on abstract review?▼

OpenWater handles application-style review well. Sessionboard adds med/sci chair tools, conflict of interest (COI), speaker readiness after acceptance, and the content stack that turns sessions into searchable assets — not only committee scores.

We left CTI for OpenWater — why look again?▼

Migration fixed the vendor name, not always the workflow. If speakers still live in email and winners still re-enter as faculty, the program layer was never solved.

Does Sessionboard replace Award Force or Evalato too?▼

For associations that also run a meeting, yes — those tools stop at the awards season. Pure awards-only buyers with no conference overlap may still prefer a specialist; association buyers usually do not stay awards-only for long.
