## Quick answer

Submittable is a submission and review platform for grants, fellowships, and awards programs. Sessionboard Awards targets associations where winners join conference faculty, judges serve across cycles, and marketing needs Advocacy with attribution — not a submission export. Submittable fits horizontal grants intake; Sessionboard fits event-native program graphs.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Grants and awards manager

Submittable closes the review cycle — winners re-entered manually into conference systems.

### Conference program director

Fellowship recipients should present — one faculty record from award acceptance to speaker portal.

### Marketing director

Winner announcements without tracked registration links — Advocacy belongs on the program graph.

### Research society operations

Abstract authors and award nominees are the same people — CRM should not reset per program.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where Submittable stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## Submittable — where it ends

- **Horizontal submission platform**
Grants, fellowships, and awards across nonprofit and corporate use cases.

- **Review workflow flexibility**
Configurable forms and review stages for varied programs.

- **Non-event grants focus**
Built for standalone submissions, so it fits only when no conference program ties to outcomes.


### The narrow case

- Grants or fellowships with no conference or speaker program
- Submission intake is the only digital workflow required

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Event-native speaker CRM**
Nominees, judges, winners, and session faculty on one graph — year over year.

- **Conference plus awards**
Call for papers and awards share review patterns and faculty database.

- **Post-award content and Advocacy**
Ceremony sessions, clips, and tracked winner promotion on session records.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Award winners routinely join conference agenda or marketing campaigns
- Same SME pool serves as abstract authors, judges, and advocates
- Portfolio needs event-over-event reporting on faculty and advocates

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.
