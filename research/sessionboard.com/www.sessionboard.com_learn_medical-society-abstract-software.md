## Vendor landscape

No single winner — match the tool to your trigger, data ownership, and team workflow.

| CTI / cOASIS | A single anchor congress at very large scale runs on analyst-led configuration and the society wants a services relationship rather than self-service software. | Mid-season form and report changes wait on a vendor queue, and speaker operations after acceptance live outside the system. | Services-led, scoped per congress. |

| OpenWater | Abstracts share a contract with awards and grants, and forms-plus-review covers the whole requirement. | Post-acceptance workflow, faculty history, and content capture need other tools. | Priced by module breadth. |

## Evaluation checklist

- Does blind review support your actual policy, single, double, or committee-tiered, with conflict-of-interest tracking built in?
- Are disclosures collected in the same system as submissions, or reconciled by staff across two exports?
- Can chairs change forms, rubrics, and reports themselves during review season?
- Do faculty records persist across annual meetings, so returning presenters see prefilled profiles?
- What runs after acceptance? Speaker portal, AV specs, upload deadlines, and automated reminders, or a staff inbox?
- Are sessions captured? Transcription and a media library extend the meeting's value past the closing session.
- If members must authenticate, does the platform support SSO against your AMS?

## Frequently asked questions

What does medical society abstract software need that generic submission tools skip?▼

Disclosure and conflict-of-interest workflows, committee-tiered blind review, AMS single sign-on for members, and faculty records that persist across years. Generic form tools handle submission; societies need the review governance around it.

Does Sessionboard handle disclosures and COI?▼

Yes, natively: disclosure collection on submission, conflict-of-interest tracking through review, and blind review modes that match society policy. Bring your current workflow to a demo and see it rebuilt live.

We run our congress on CTI today. What does a move look like?▼

In-flight cycles finish where they are. The next call for papers opens in Sessionboard, faculty and acceptance history import, and the committee configures its own forms from day one instead of filing tickets.

Can the abstract system feed our meeting content strategy?▼

That is the point of running it on one record. Sessions carry transcription, captions, and clips, so the program your committee built becomes a member-facing library instead of ending at the closing session.

Do reviewers and awards judges need separate accounts?▼

Not on a platform with one contact graph. Reviewers, judges, faculty, and submitters share identity in Sessionboard, so a member who reviews abstracts and judges awards signs in once and staff see one record.
