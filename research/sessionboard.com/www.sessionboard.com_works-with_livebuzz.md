## Sessionboard — built in

- **Abstract and reviewer operations**
Multi-round review, COI, and chair self-service before agenda publish.

- **Authoritative program graph**
Speaker CRM, agenda truth, and session metadata — pushed to LiveBuzz, not re-keyed.

- **Content Marketing stack**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Create, and Amplify on session records after show day.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from LiveBuzz.

- Program team runs CFP and review in Sessionboard — accepted speakers and sessions are authoritative.
- Native LiveBuzz connector pushes speakers → Customers, sessions → Seminars, theaters → Theaters (REST v3).
- LiveBuzz registration and event app consume synced program data — delegates see current agenda in LiveControl.
- Post-event transcription, clips, and Attendee Hub stay on Sessionboard session records.

**Native connector:** LiveBuzz connector shipped on Sessionboard apps-engine (SB-7670) — Bearer auth + BUZZ-Version header, scheduled sync, source/source\_id idempotency. Confirm campaign credentials and IP allowlisting with sales engineering.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep LiveBuzz only when

- Marketing and ops standardized on LiveBuzz LiveControl for registration and event app this season
- You want LiveBuzz attendee UX plus Sessionboard abstract committee and speaker CRM
- UK or international conference with LiveBuzz as registration and content-module vendor

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Abstract committee pain is the bottleneck — not registration UX
- Need transcription, clips, and advocacy tied to program sessions beyond LiveBuzz content modules
- Speaker identity must persist across years on one CRM independent of LiveControl campaigns

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside LiveBuzz.

Does Sessionboard replace LiveBuzz?▼

Not by default — coexistence on registration and event app is the common pattern. Sessionboard replaces spreadsheet program ops and owns content capture LiveBuzz content modules do not cover.

Is there a native integration?▼

Yes — apps-engine LiveBuzz connector pushes speakers, sessions, and theaters to LiveControl API v3. Confirm campaign ID, API key, and allowlisting for your account.

Who owns registration data?▼

LiveBuzz LiveControl remains system of record for tickets and attendee reg. Sessionboard owns committee workflow, speakers, sessions, and post-event content.

What syncs outbound today?▼

Speakers, sessions (seminars), and theaters — with source\_id linking for create-vs-update. Custom fields and badge streams may require additional handler work; confirm with sales engineering.

Can we use Sessionboard without LiveBuzz registration?▼

Yes — many teams use Sessionboard as the program layer beside other registration vendors. This page is for teams already on or evaluating LiveBuzz LiveControl.
