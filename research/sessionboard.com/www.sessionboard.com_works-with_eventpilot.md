## Quick answer

EventPilot provides event management tooling for associations and professional societies. Sessionboard integrates as the program layer — call for papers, multi-round review, speaker portal, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy — when EventPilot handles broader event operations and teams need deeper committee and faculty workflows.

Event workflows societies associate with EventPilot deployments.

## Sessionboard — built in

- **Abstract and speaker program**
CFP, review, portal, and agenda on one graph.

- **Speaker CRM year over year**
Faculty and SME database across annual cycles.

- **Content marketing**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Advocacy beside EventPilot operations.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from EventPilot.

- Sessionboard runs CFP through speaker readiness — authoritative program graph.
- Session and speaker data sync or export to EventPilot event records.
- Marketing and Advocacy fire from Sessionboard acceptance state.
- Post-event content persists in Sessionboard Media Library.

**Integration status:** Coexistence model — Sessionboard as program layer beside EventPilot; confirm sync paths on your demo call.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep EventPilot only when

- EventPilot already deployed for association event operations
- Abstract volume or review complexity exceeds spreadsheet tolerance

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Simple single-track meeting with no formal review
- No post-event content or faculty promotion requirements
