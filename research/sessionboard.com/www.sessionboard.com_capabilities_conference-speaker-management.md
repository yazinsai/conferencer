## One system to manage speakers, sessions, and everything in between

Sessionboard replaces disconnected tools and inbox chaos with one system for submissions, reviews, speaker comms, and agenda planning. Less back and forth. More progress.

### **Speaker CRM**

Turn your speaker network into a marketing engine

Sessionboard's Speaker CRM helps you find, manage, and activate your network of experts. You can source the right voices, fuel more impactful content, and scale trust across every channel.

![lennd-portal](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/627d5c68d5739644034cfd28/656571f7c65d4d9d5cc93818_sb-advanced-search-min.webp)

### **Call for Papers**

Collect, track, and review session proposals without the manual overhead

Create multiple branded submission forms in minutes. Track draft proposals, assign reviewers, and send targeted reminders as needed. Sessionboard helps you manage high-volume session workflows with setup that takes minutes and tracking that doesn’t miss a step.

### **Submission Evaluation**

Score and review sessions without the back and forth

Sessionboard gives you a faster way to review and select top sessions. Assign proposals, collect scores, and use AI-generated evaluations to support your team with consistent, persona-based scoring. Whether you use a rubric or freeform feedback, you’ll get aligned decisions without back and forth.

![lennd-dashboard](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/627d5c68d5739644034cfd28/685de2aa9376bbea5855cdc3_9e366f5bd102af7486fa330eba1c3d06_NEW-Homepage-Submission%20Evals%20.webp)

![lennd-portal](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/627d5c68d5739644034cfd28/685de35e57949c839bffa0e9_NEW-Homepage-Agenda.webp)

### **Manage Sessions and Agendas**

Assign sessions confidently

When session planning is done in static tools, it’s hard to see overlaps, gaps, or room issues until it’s too late. Sessionboard gives you a clear, flexible view of your schedule so you can place sessions, resolve conflicts, and keep everything on track.

### **Personalized Onboarding Portals**

Give each speaker a clear path from submission to showtime

Without structure, onboarding turns into endless follow ups and missing files. Sessionboard gives every speaker a personalized, branded portal with tasks, content, and deadlines so nothing slips through and everyone shows up prepared.

![lennd-dashboard](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/627d5c68d5739644034cfd28/685de3a1a521b337a4bc0a52_NEW%20Personalized%20Onboarding%20Portals.webp)

![lennd-portal](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/627d5c68d5739644034cfd28/685de3df9fba338ca6c82dc0_NEW-Homepage-Dynamic%20Dashboards%20and%20Comms.webp)

### **Dynamic Dashboards and Communication**

Stay organized and keep speakers on track

Track the status of every speaker with clear dashboards. Set reminders, send messages, and manage tasks from one place. No back and forth or missed steps, just a smoother process for your team and your speakers.

### **Content Management**

Event-ready content management built for speaker files

Managing event content is painful when decks, bios, and videos are scattered across tools. Sessionboard lets you track files by session or speaker, add comments as needed, and download everything in organized batches by room, track, or day. No guessing. No last-minute file hunts.
