## Where enterprise events actually break

Registration suites excel at tickets and badges. Program committees still live in email, PDFs, and spreadsheets between acceptance and show day.

- Call for papers opens on one system; review happens in inboxes — scores and comments lost when chairs rotate.
- Speakers submit materials through ad hoc forms; ops teams cannot see who is blocking program publish.
- Registration platforms hold attendee records; faculty CRM and multi-year speaker history reset each event.
- Last-minute substitutions force manual agenda rebuilds without audit trail of what changed and why.

## How Sessionboard runs the program layer

One system for reviewers, chairs, speakers, and agenda state — synced beside your registration stack.

- Call for papers published → configurable forms, tracks, and submission rules — changes without a services ticket.
- Review rounds open → reviewers score in-system; chairs see conflict of interest declarations and assignment load.
- Decisions recorded → accepted abstracts flow to speaker portal tasks — bio, headshot, slides, and consent on one checklist.
- Program build → sessions, tracks, rooms, and conflicts managed on the acceptance graph — not a separate spreadsheet.
- Registration sync → speaker and session data export or integrate to Cvent, RainFocus, and other stacks documented on works-with pages.

## Frequently asked questions

Program operations beside registration — not a rip-and-replace migration.

Is Sessionboard abstract submission software?▼

Yes — and that is the front third of it. Authors submit through custom forms, reviewers score in multi-round blind workflows with COI checks, and then acceptance flows into speaker portal, materials, and program build. Submission-only tools stop at the decision; Sessionboard runs the whole cycle on one record.

Do we replace our registration platform?▼

Usually not. Sessionboard owns call for papers, review, speaker ops, and program build. Registration, badges, and housing stay on Cvent, Cadmium, RainFocus, or your AMS.

How many review rounds can we run?▼

Multi-round workflows with chair assignments and scoring rubrics are core — built for medical and scientific committee calendars, not single-pass submission tools.

Can we change forms mid-cycle?▼

Program teams need self-service CFP and review configuration — not a ticket queue for every field change during an open call for papers.

What about conflict of interest?▼

Reviewer COI capture and assignment rules are part of committee workflow — critical for accredited medical meetings.

How does speaker data reach registration?▼

Sync and export paths depend on your stack — see works-with guides for Cvent, RainFocus, and other platforms. The goal is one faculty record in Sessionboard, not double entry.

Is this only for in-person events?▼

Virtual and hybrid sessions use the same abstract and speaker graph — webinar runtime is documented separately on works-with pages.

When should we stay with registration alone?▼

Single-track internal meetings under 50 speakers with no formal review rarely need a program layer. Add Sessionboard when committee pain is unprompted in leadership meetings.
