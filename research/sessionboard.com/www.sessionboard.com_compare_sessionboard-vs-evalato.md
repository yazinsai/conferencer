## Quick answer

Evalato is a submission and awards platform for grants, fellowships, and association awards — forms, review stages, and winner communications. Sessionboard Awards targets event programs where winners join conference faculty, judges return next season, and Advocacy needs registration attribution. Evalato fits horizontal awards intake; Sessionboard fits conference plus awards on one program graph with SME database year over year.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where Evalato stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## Evalato — where it ends

- **Grants and awards breadth**
Horizontal submission platform across nonprofit and corporate programs.

- **Review workflow builder**
Flexible stages and reviewer assignments for varied award types.

- **Non-event awards focus**
Built for standalone awards, so it fits only when outcomes never tie to a conference program.


### The narrow case

- Grants and awards with no conference speaker or content requirements
- Multiple unrelated submission programs on one horizontal contract

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Event-native speaker CRM**
Winners, judges, nominees, and session faculty on one graph — persists event over event.

- **Conference plus awards wedge**
Enter via Awards when conference contract is locked; expand to call for papers later.

- **Advocacy and live content**
Tracked shares, transcription, and Attendee Hub on winner and ceremony sessions.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Winners routinely present at or promote the annual meeting
- Judges and abstract reviewers should share identity and history
- Marketing needs attributed faculty and winner promotion
- Portfolio needs YoY SME and advocate reporting

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.
