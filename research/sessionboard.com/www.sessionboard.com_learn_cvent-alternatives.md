## Evaluation checklist

- Which Cvent module triggered the search — registration cost, the mobile app, or the speaker and abstract workflow? Replace that job, not the suite.
- Can the program committee change forms, review workflows, and reports without a vendor ticket or a services queue?
- Does speaker and faculty history persist across editions, or does each event start from a fresh import?
- What happens after acceptance — does the platform run speaker readiness, AV specs, and material uploads, or hand you back to email?
- Is session content captured and reusable — transcription, clips, and a media library — or does the program evaporate when the event ends?
- If registration stays on Cvent, does the alternative run cleanly beside it with clear ownership of the program record?
- Compare the unbundled total (registration plus program layer) against the suite renewal — line by line, not list price.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Cvent?▼

Depends on the job. For registration, teams shortlist Swoogo and Bizzabo. For enterprise portfolio data, RainFocus. For the speaker, abstract, and session content layer — the module program teams outgrow first — Sessionboard runs call for papers, review, speaker portal, transcription, and awards beside whichever registration platform you keep.

Do we have to replace all of Cvent at once?▼

No, and most teams shouldn't. Registration contracts and program operations run on different calendars and serve different owners. Unbundling one job — usually the speaker and abstract side — de-risks the change and gives you leverage at the next registration renewal.

Is Sessionboard a Cvent competitor?▼

For speaker, abstract, and content management, yes — that is the lane where Sessionboard goes deeper than the suite: multi-round blind review, speaker CRM across years, session transcription, and awards on one record. For registration and venue sourcing, no; Sessionboard runs beside Cvent or any alternative.

What do teams outgrow first in Cvent?▼

Usually the speaker and abstract workflow. Complex programs need multi-round review, conflict-of-interest tracking, speaker readiness, and content capture — jobs the suite covers thinly — while registration keeps working fine. That is why replacing the program layer first is the common move.

Can Sessionboard run beside Cvent registration?▼

Yes. Registration stays where it is; Sessionboard owns the program record — submissions, review, speakers, materials, transcription — and the two sides exchange the data they need. Many customers run exactly this split.

How do the alternatives compare on price?▼

Swoogo publishes flat annual licensing; the others quote by scope. The honest comparison is your suite renewal against the unbundled stack you'd actually run — registration plus program layer — priced line by line before you sign.
