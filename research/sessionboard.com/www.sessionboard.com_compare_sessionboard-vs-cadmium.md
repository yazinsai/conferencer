## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against Cadmium for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

What is CadmiumCD?▼

CadmiumCD is the original name of the company now trading as Cadmium, whose product line includes eventScribe for the attendee app, Abstract Scorecard for abstract collection and review, Conference Harvester for speaker and presentation collection, and Elevate and EthosCE for continuing education. Many teams still search the old product names because that is what their contracts say.

What replaces Abstract Scorecard?▼

Sessionboard covers the same job — call for papers, submission, and scored review — and continues past the decision into speaker portal, materials collection, scheduling, and poster sessions on one record. Abstract Scorecard hands the program build back to you; that handoff is where most committee time disappears.

What replaces Conference Harvester?▼

Conference Harvester collects presentations, bios, and disclosures from confirmed speakers. Sessionboard does that from the same record the abstract was accepted on, so presenters are not asked to re-enter details they already submitted, and chasing is automated rather than manual.

Do we have to replace Cadmium entirely?▼

No. Most teams add Sessionboard for the program layer while registration and CE credit stay on Cadmium. Replacing eventScribe or the exhibit modules is rarely the reason anyone calls us.

What about CE credits on EthosCE or Elevate?▼

CE credit reporting stays on your learning management bundle. Sessionboard handles abstract intake, speakers, and live content — and can feed learning-object data from transcripts where configured.

Can we run one meeting on Sessionboard first?▼

Yes. One annual meeting or specialty track is the common entry point beside an existing registration stack.

Does Sessionboard transcribe and repurpose sessions?▼

Yes — live and batch transcription, quote extraction, clips, and Create outputs are native. No separate Conference AI subscription required.

What happens to session content after the meeting?▼

Each captured session gets an AI recap an admin reviews, edits, and publishes to a branded web page attendees and faculty can share. Publishing also files the session's best quotes into your quote library, so next cycle's marketing starts from a full corpus instead of AV folders.

How does Sessionboard compare on pricing?▼

Pricing follows meeting and abstract volume — request a demo for a scoped quote.
