## Quick answer

Sessionboard competes with Cvent on the speaker and content layer only — abstract review, speaker readiness, transcription, Attendee Hub, and advocacy on a session-forward graph. Cvent registration and event core stay in place for 28+ coexistence customers; this is not a suite rip-and-replace.

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Program director

Cvent speaker module stops at lists and exports — you need committee depth and chair self-service on 500+ abstracts.

### Speaker readiness lead

Speaker portal and materials live outside Cvent — you need one workflow from acceptance through on-site check-in.

### Marketing director

Cvent share bar shows clicks, not registrations — you need Advocacy attribution from program events and Dispatch for staff.

### AV / content producer

Transcripts and clips in a separate vendor — you need capture, Attendee Hub, and Create tied to the session graph.

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Program-forward data model**
Sessions, reviewers, and speakers — not attendee-record backward.

- **Med/sci committee depth**
Multi-round review, conflict of interest (COI) capture, and chair self-service at 500+ abstracts.

- **Content Marketing built in**
Transcription, clips, Attendee Hub, Create, and Advocacy on the session graph.

- **Advocacy & Dispatch**
Advocacy — tracked peer shares from speakers and sponsors with registration attribution. Dispatch — internal team share recipes when content is ready — not Cvent generic share buttons.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Program team still uses spreadsheets beside Cvent speaker module

- Share-to-registration attribution required — not generic share bar

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.
