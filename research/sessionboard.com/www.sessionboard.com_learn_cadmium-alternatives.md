## Quick answer

Teams looking at Cadmium alternatives usually want committee self-service instead of vendor configuration, a speaker CRM that persists across years, and content capture on the session record. Sessionboard covers all three plus call for papers, review, and awards. OpenWater fits form-based programs, CTI/cOASIS fits analyst-run congresses, Ex Ordo fits review-only scope.

## Evaluation checklist

- Can the program committee change forms, workflows, and reports the day they need to, without a vendor ticket?
- Does faculty history (bios, materials, participation, consent) persist across editions, or does each year start from a blank import?
- What happens after acceptance? Does the platform run speaker readiness, AV specs, and uploads, or hand you back to email?
- Is session content captured? Transcription, captions, and a media library turn the program into a year-round asset.
- If you run awards beside the meeting, do nominees and judges share identity with your abstract and faculty records?
- How does the vendor handle mid-cycle migration when review rounds are already in flight?

## Frequently asked questions

What is the most common reason teams leave Cadmium?▼

Configuration dependence. eventScribe changes route through the vendor, so committees wait on a queue for form tweaks, report changes, and workflow updates during the busiest weeks of the season. Self-service platforms remove the wait.

Is Sessionboard a full Cadmium replacement for abstracts?▼

For abstract and speaker operations, yes: call for papers, custom forms, multi-round blind review, conflict-of-interest tracking, speaker portal, and transcription run natively. Registration and CE stay on your current stack, and Sessionboard runs beside them.

Can we switch mid-cycle?▼

In-flight review rounds can finish where they are while the next cycle opens in the new system. Accepted submissions and faculty records import, so returning speakers see prefilled profiles instead of a blank form.

How do these options compare on price?▼

None of the four publish list pricing; quotes follow scope and services. Put a scoped Sessionboard quote beside your Cadmium renewal before you sign it, the demo takes an hour.

What happens to our faculty history from past eventScribe years?▼

It becomes the starting point. Past participation, bios, and materials import into the speaker CRM, so returning faculty see prefilled profiles and the committee sees who delivered across editions.
