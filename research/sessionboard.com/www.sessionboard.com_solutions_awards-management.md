## Quick answer

Sessionboard runs awards judging as multi-round scoring: judges assigned by category with conflicts excluded, scoring against a written rubric, and a shortlist round before a deeper pass on finalists. Judges, nominees and winners persist as records across cycles rather than resetting each season. Award Force, Evalato, and OpenWater excel at narrow judging-season scope; Sessionboard fits when identity should carry year over year and a conference tie-in matters.

## Why awards programs reset every cycle

Standalone awards tools handle judging seasons well — then winner lists export to spreadsheets while conference faculty live somewhere else.

- Awards season opens fresh each year — prior judges, nominees, and winners do not carry forward as CRM records.
- Winners exported to Excel before conference speaker lists are built — duplicate data entry and stale bios.
- Grants and conference abstracts on different contracts — no shared reviewer pool or faculty history.
- Ceremony content and winner clips live outside the awards platform — no tie to Attendee Hub or post-event reuse.

## Frequently asked questions

Awards on Sessionboard — connected to abstract review, speaker ops, and content marketing.

Does Sessionboard replace AwardForce or Evessio?▼

When you want robust awards with member SSO, dedupe, merge, and optional conference on one CRM — yes. Point tools may stay if you need only a single judging season with no identity graph.

Can we run awards only?▼

Yes — many customers run Awards without a conference. SSO for members, submission dedupe and merge, and multi-round judging work standalone; add call for papers or speaker ops on the same graph when ready.

Do judges and abstract reviewers share workflows?▼

Same chair tools, rubrics, and assignment patterns — one platform skill set for program volunteers.

What happens to winners after the ceremony?▼

Winner sessions, clips, and transcripts live on the same records — Attendee Hub and Create for post-event reuse.

How does year-over-year reporting work?▼

Advocate, submission, and faculty metrics roll up across events for portfolio and board reporting.
