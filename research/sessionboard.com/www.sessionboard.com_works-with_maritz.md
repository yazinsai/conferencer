## Maritz Global Events — their lane

- **Registration and housing**
Attendee registration, housing blocks, and operational reg services through Maritz / Experient.

- **Full-service event management**
Sourcing, on-site staffing, and association conference planning at portfolio scale.

- **Registration system of record**
Show codes, registrant records, and contracted event services remain on Maritz for many annual meetings.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Abstract and reviewer operations**
Multi-round review, COI, and chair self-service on the program graph.

- **Speaker CRM through show day**
Materials, bios, and agenda truth — not re-keyed from registration exports.

- **Content Marketing stack**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Create, and Amplify on session records.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Maritz Global Events.

- Attendees register through Maritz / Experient — registration record stays on Maritz.
- Accepted speakers and sessions live in Sessionboard — program team works without spreadsheet exports.
- Session and speaker data align via scheduled export or RTS/API integration — confirm field map with sales engineering.
- Live transcription and Attendee Hub deliver content from Sessionboard session records.

**Integration status:** Maritz Real Time Services (RTS) and program import paths are available for many accounts — confirm connector scope and show-code credentials with Sessionboard sales engineering.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep Maritz Global Events only when

- Multi-year Maritz registration or full-service contract for the annual meeting
- Ops team standardized on Maritz / Experient for attendee processing and housing
- PCO or association staff wants Maritz for reg while program committee needs modern abstract ops

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Abstract committee still runs in email and Excel beside Maritz registration
- Need transcription, clips, and advocacy tied to program sessions
- Speaker identity must persist across years on one CRM
- Evaluating displacement from legacy abstract systems (see Sessionboard vs CTI compare)

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside Maritz Global Events.

Does Sessionboard replace Maritz?▼

No — registration coexistence is the common pattern. Sessionboard replaces spreadsheet program ops and legacy abstract workflows beside Maritz registration and event services.

Is Experient the same as Maritz?▼

Experient registration technology is part of Maritz Global Events. This page covers coexistence with Maritz registration regardless of which brand name is on your contract.

Is there a native integration?▼

Confirm RTS/API or import scope with Sessionboard sales — program layer can start via import while registration stays on Maritz.

Who owns registration data?▼

Maritz remains system of record for tickets, housing, and attendee reg. Sessionboard owns sessions, speakers, and content.
