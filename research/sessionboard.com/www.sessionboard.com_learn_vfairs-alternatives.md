## Evaluation checklist

- Name the layer that actually broke — logistics UX, pricing, or program depth — before touring any demo
- Test the abstract module at your real volume: multi-round review, blind modes, COI rules, and a mid-cycle form change, not the five-abstract demo
- Ask where speaker data lives after the event: one CRM record across years, or a profile reset per event instance
- Ask what members and attendees can reach in month six: session library, poster gallery, or nothing
- Price the layers separately: a bundle discount on registration does not offset committee software you still have to run in spreadsheets
- Check exit paths: your abstracts, reviews, and speaker records should export cleanly whichever direction you go

## Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to vFairs?▼

It depends on the layer you are replacing. Cvent for enterprise registration and venue sourcing, Whova for mobile-app-first events on budget, Swoogo for registration flexibility, and Sessionboard for the program layer: abstract review, speaker operations, and event content. There is no like-for-like clone of the full bundle.

Who are vFairs' main competitors?▼

Cvent, Whova, Swoogo, Bizzabo, and EventMobi compete on the logistics and app side; Hopin's successors compete on virtual venues. On the abstract and speaker layer, the depth competitors are Sessionboard, Cadmium, and Oxford Abstracts rather than the suites.

Do I have to replace vFairs entirely?▼

No, and most teams shouldn't. Registration, badging, and the mobile app are commonly kept. The pattern that works is adding depth on the layer that broke — usually abstracts, speakers, and content — while the logistics bundle stays.

Is the vFairs abstract management module enough for a medical meeting?▼

For a light call for papers, yes. For committee-run review at volume — blind rounds, conflict-of-interest rules, chair assignments, rubric scoring, mid-cycle changes — teams typically outgrow it and run the real workflow in spreadsheets beside the platform, which is the point where a program-native layer earns its keep.

How does vFairs pricing work?▼

Custom quotes based on event format (virtual, hybrid, in-person), selected features, and attendee volume, offered as annual licenses or per-event packages. Get the program-layer modules priced separately in any quote so you can compare them against dedicated tools honestly.

Can Sessionboard run beside vFairs?▼

Yes. vFairs keeps registration, badging, the app, and the venue; Sessionboard runs call for papers, committee review, speaker CRM, poster galleries, and the post-event content library on one program record, syncing beside the logistics stack.
