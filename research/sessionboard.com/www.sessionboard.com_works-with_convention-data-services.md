## Convention Data Services — their lane

- **Association registration**
Attendee registration, badges, and operational reg services on CDSreg.

- **Show operations familiarity**
Teams and PCOs trained on CDS workflows for recurring annual meetings.

- **Registration system of record**
Tickets and attendee records remain on CDS for contracted events.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Abstract and reviewer operations**
Multi-round review, COI, and chair self-service on the program graph.

- **Speaker CRM through show day**
Materials, bios, and agenda truth — not re-keyed from registration exports.

- **Content Marketing stack**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Create, and Amplify on session records.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Convention Data Services.

- Attendees register through CDSreg — registration record stays on CDS.
- Accepted speakers and sessions live in Sessionboard — program team works without spreadsheet exports.
- Agenda and speaker data align via scheduled export or integration — confirm with sales.
- Live transcription and Attendee Hub deliver content from Sessionboard session records.

**Integration status:** CDSreg ↔ Sessionboard — program import paths available; confirm connector scope for your account with Sessionboard sales engineering.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep Convention Data Services only when

- Multi-year CDS registration contract for the annual meeting
- Ops team standardized on CDSreg for attendee processing

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Abstract committee still runs in email and Excel after CDS export
- Need transcription, clips, and advocacy tied to program sessions
- Speaker identity must persist across years on one CRM
