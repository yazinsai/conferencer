## Quick answer

ePosterBoards is a rental service — monitors, kiosks, and onsite technicians for the physical poster hall. Sessionboard is the program software: call for papers, committee review, author uploads with consent, a searchable published gallery on one link, and per-poster engagement analytics. Many meetings rent screens onsite and run the program on Sessionboard.

Consent, disclosures, and withdrawals are your problem — hardware vendors don't touch compliance.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where ePosterBoards stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## ePosterBoards — where it ends

- **Onsite hardware and logistics**
Large-format monitors, kiosk builds, stands, shipping, setup and breakdown labor for the physical hall.

- **Onsite technical staffing**
Technicians in the room handling last-minute uploads, formatting fixes, and presenter support during sessions.

- **Presenter formatting help**
Pre-conference templates and formatting assistance for PowerPoint, Keynote, and Prezi ePosters.


### The narrow case

- The buy is physical hall equipment — monitors, kiosks, and onsite staffing for the session days
- Presenters bring finished slides on a flash drive and the program lives entirely elsewhere
- One-off meeting with no requirement for a persistent online gallery or engagement reporting

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Posters on the program graph**
Poster tracks in call for papers, shared multi-round review with platform abstracts, and program numbers that stay current — no re-import.

- **Published gallery on one link**
A branded, searchable virtual poster hall attendees open from anywhere — before, during, and after the meeting, not just in the room.

- **Consent and compliance in the product**
Versioned publication consent at upload, disclosure display, embargo with teaser mode, and withdrawn abstracts excluded automatically.

- **Engagement your committee can read**
Poster opens, dwell time, most-viewed posters, author questions, and zero-result searches per gallery.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Posters share a call for papers with platform sessions and need the same review rounds
- The society wants a searchable online poster hall members can open all year, not only hall hours
- Consent, disclosures, embargo, or withdrawal handling must be enforced before anything publishes
- The committee expects engagement data — views, dwell time, and what attendees searched for

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against ePosterBoards for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Is Sessionboard a replacement for ePosterBoards?▼

They cover different layers. ePosterBoards rents onsite display hardware with technicians; Sessionboard runs the poster program — acceptance, uploads, consent, the published gallery, and analytics. Meetings that want rented screens onsite often run both, with Sessionboard's gallery on the kiosk browser and on every attendee's phone.

Can Sessionboard's gallery run on onsite screens?▼

Yes. The gallery is a web link, so it runs on any monitor, kiosk browser, or attendee device. QR signage connects the physical hall to the same gallery remote attendees use.

What happens to the poster program after the meeting?▼

It stays live. The gallery persists after the hall closes, members browse it year-round, and multi-event organizers keep a poster library that carries across annual meetings.

How do posters get into the gallery?▼

Authors upload in the same speaker portal they use for bios and headshots. Accepted abstracts already carry titles, authors, and program numbers, so there is no spreadsheet import step.

Does Sessionboard handle consent and disclosures?▼

Yes — versioned publication consent at upload, disclosure fields on the poster page, embargo with teaser mode, and automatic exclusion of withdrawn abstracts.

How does pricing compare?▼

ePosterBoards prices as an equipment and staffing rental per meeting. Sessionboard pricing follows meeting and submission volume — request a demo for a scoped quote.
