## Why generic advocacy fails at events

Horizontal tools optimize staff shares and template campaigns — not the full advocate mix around a conference or trade show. Sessionboard Advocacy and Dispatch cover every participant type and content format on the program graph you already run.

- Bolt-on P2P tools import advocate lists after program data already lives elsewhere — speakers, exhibitors, and sponsors get generic assets, not session-linked pages.
- Internal teams (membership, sales, GTM) share from spreadsheets or Slack threads — no tracked links, no rollup with external advocate performance.
- Share metrics without registration attribution leave marketing guessing which posts drove attendance — clicks and conversions must tie to advocate and channel.
- Manual nudge emails and Slack reminders don't scale — advocates need personalized, milestone-timed prompts from an Advocacy Agent, not a copy-paste template your ops team sends one by one.
- Event-over-event reporting is missing — each show resets campaigns instead of compounding a database of SMEs, top advocates, and what converted last year.
- Content is siloed by format — session promos, booth invites, clips, and registration pushes need one distribution layer, not separate tools per asset type.

## Frequently asked questions

Peer-to-peer and community marketing — not association government relations.

Which advocate types can use Sessionboard?▼

Advocacy covers external participants — speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees — with personalized tracked links. Dispatch covers internal teams: membership marketing, chapter volunteers, sales, GTM, and corporate comms pushing approved event content. Same program graph, same attribution layer.

What content can we distribute?▼

Any asset tied to the event program — session and speaker promos, registration and early-bird URLs, agenda highlights, video clips, transcript quote cards, sponsor kits, and post-event on-demand links. Advocacy and Dispatch assign tracked links per advocate and content type.

How does event-over-event reporting work?▼

Advocate performance, channel mix, share events, clicks, and attributed registrations roll up across events in your portfolio — compare what worked last year, which SMEs and sponsors drove conversion, and which channels to invest in for the next cycle.

Does this build an advocate or SME database?▼

Yes — speaker CRM and advocate records persist across annual events. Faculty, presenters, top-performing exhibitors, and repeat promoters carry forward so each show starts with a known graph of SMEs and advocates, not a cold import every season.

What is the Advocacy Agent?▼

Sessionboard's Advocacy Agent sends personalized, milestone-timed nudges to speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, attendees, and other advocates — pre-filled share assets and tracked links from program state. It keeps advocates sharing and re-activates quiet ones without manual follow-up emails.

What is the difference between Advocacy and Dispatch?▼

Advocacy is peer and community marketing for event participants — external advocates with session-linked assets. Dispatch is internal team distribution for membership, sales, GTM, and marketing staff when program content is ready to push.

Is this employee advocacy?▼

Advocacy is primarily event participants promoting sessions they are in — not payroll headcount. Dispatch covers internal staff for the event window. Generic employee-advocacy platforms report sub-10% participation; event-native motive is the wedge.

How does this compare to Gleanin, Snöball, or InGo?▼

Those tools excel at campaign UX for external advocates. Sessionboard adds program-native triggers, any advocate type including internal teams via Dispatch, content from transcripts, registration attribution, and event-over-event rollup on the same graph as CFP and speaker ops. Coexist or consolidate when attribution and SME depth matter more than a standalone widget.

Can we resell amplification to sponsors?▼

Yes — package exhibitor and sponsor Advocacy campaigns with per-link reporting in the sponsorship contract. Measurable inventory beats one-off organiser posts on social channels.

What do we measure?▼

Attributed registrations and conversions per advocate, channel, and content asset — plus portfolio rollup across events for year-round marketing decisions.
