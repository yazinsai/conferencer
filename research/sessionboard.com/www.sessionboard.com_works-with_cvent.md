Cvent serves registration-heavy enterprises with cvent webcasts and event marketing. Sessionboard is the webinar program layer: request forms, SME Explorer across your speaker CRM, recording and transcript ingest, clips, Advocacy to hosts and attendees, Dispatch to internal teams, and efficacy back to CRM — with a shipped app connector.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Head of webinar marketing

Teams request webinars faster than you can find SMEs — intake forms and SME Explorer should rank experts from your full speaker CRM, not Slack threads.

### Content marketing

Recordings die in Cvent after live — transcription, clips, and Create should live on the same graph as conference sessions.

### Membership or community lead

Internal teams need ready-to-send packs — Dispatch routes clips and talking points to membership, chapters, and customer success without manual email chains.

### GTM and sales enablement

Field teams miss webinar moments — Dispatch delivers tracked clip packs; Advocacy lets hosts and panelists share with attribution.

## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Cvent.

- A team submits a webinar request — or marketing schedules one already on the runtime.
- SME Explorer surfaces ranked experts from your speaker CRM across annual meetings and past webinars.
- Live delivery stays on Cvent; Scheduled and completed webinars sync from Cvent into Sessionboard Webinars.
- Transcription and Create produce clips; Advocacy activates for external audiences; Dispatch sends internal packs.
- Shares, clicks, and influenced registrations roll up to CRM so program efficacy is visible.

**Native connector:** Cvent registration sync and webinar/webcast schedule sync are available as Sessionboard connectors — program layer beside your Cvent contract.

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- You run a high-volume webinar program — not one-off broadcasts
- SME discovery and assignment is the bottleneck, not live streaming
- Advocacy and Dispatch participation should be measured, not manual "please share" emails
- Conference speakers and webinar hosts should share one CRM graph

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside Cvent.

Does Sessionboard replace Cvent?▼

No for live webinar delivery. Cvent stays the runtime; Sessionboard owns request intake, SME matching, recording ingest, clips, Advocacy, Dispatch, and measurement.

How do teams find SMEs for the next webinar?▼

Webinar request forms feed SME Explorer — search and rank experts from your speaker CRM across every event, past webinars, topics, and engagement.

How does Cvent connect to Sessionboard?▼

Cvent is available as a Sessionboard app connector. Scheduled webinars, recordings, presenters, and engagement signals sync into the Webinars module and Media Library.

What is the difference between Advocacy and Dispatch?▼

Advocacy reaches external audiences — hosts, panelists, and attendees — with tracked share links. Dispatch delivers ready-to-send clip packs to internal teams: membership, GTM, sales, and customer success.

Can we measure whether webinars drive pipeline?▼

Yes — share and click tracking per recipient rolls up to CRM contacts and companies, alongside registration attribution from Advocacy links.
