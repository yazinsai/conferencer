## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep InGo mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## InGo — their lane

- **Social advocacy campaigns**
Sharing flows and social promotion UX for speakers, exhibitors, and attendees.

- **Network reach mechanics**
Tools to extend event reach through advocate networks on social channels.

- **Marketing-owned amplification**
Teams where digital marketing runs advocacy separately from program committee ops.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Program graph**
CFP, multi-round review, speaker portal, agenda publish, and registration integration.

- **Session-linked advocacy**
Share pages built from accepted session title, abstract, bio, and headshot — not generic event blurbs.

- **Milestone-based prompts**
Advocacy tied to agenda live, early-bird, and session time — not reminder pile-ups.

- **Content and Attendee Hub**
Transcription, replays, and clips so advocacy continues post-session with watch attribution.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from InGo.

- Program team manages speakers and agenda in Sessionboard — session and speaker records are authoritative.
- Marketing optionally runs InGo campaigns using advocate lists and assets synced or exported from program data.
- InGo tracks social sharing and engagement in its advocacy layer.
- Sessionboard Advocacy generates milestone prompts and tracked links from the program graph natively.
- Registrations attribute to advocate and channel when Sessionboard owns registration — including content views on Attendee Hub.

**Integration status:** Sessionboard lists InGo as a homepage integration partner. Coexistence is the default — program depth and native Advocacy on Sessionboard; InGo for teams that keep dedicated social advocacy UX. Public API available for session and registration data exchange where custom sync is required.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep InGo only when

- Marketing contract requires InGo for this show cycle
- Social advocacy team is separate from abstract and speaker operations
- You want InGo social UX plus Sessionboard CFP, review, and speaker CRM

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Advocacy assets should generate from acceptance — not from a manual advocate import
- Attribution must tie share → registration (and optionally watch) on Sessionboard
- Speaker and exhibitor promotion should use the same session metadata committees already approved
- Post-event clips and transcripts should extend advocacy without a second content upload
