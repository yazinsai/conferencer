Where ePostersLive stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## ePostersLive — where it ends

- **ePoster hall operations**
Interactive poster display, hall statistics, and onsite kiosk patterns for large medical congresses.

- **Poster presenter experience**
Established author upload and presentation tooling for scientific poster sessions, and it stops there.

- **Med/sci poster heritage**
Long-run deployments at association meetings with heavy poster programs.


### The narrow case

- Poster hall display and interactive boards are the entire buy — no platform session or speaker CRM requirements
- Abstract review and speaker ops already contracted elsewhere and will stay siloed
- Single-format poster-only virtual fair with no formal committee workflow

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Posters on the program graph**
Poster tracks in call for papers, shared multi-round review with platform abstracts, and published agenda — not a parallel author database.

- **Speaker CRM across formats**
Portal, bio, materials, and communications for poster authors and platform faculty on one identity.

- **Attendee Hub and discovery**
Searchable poster program beside platform sessions for onsite and remote attendees.

- **Content marketing built in**
Optional live capture, Media Library, Advocacy tracked shares, and Create on the session record.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Posters share a call for papers with platform sessions and need the same review rounds
- 500+ submissions across posters and talks with chair and COI requirements
- Speaker portal, Attendee Hub, transcription, or Advocacy required after acceptance
- Registration stays on Cvent, Cadmium, or RainFocus — program layer should not stop at poster export

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against ePostersLive for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Is Sessionboard a full ePostersLive replacement?▼

ePostersLive leads on dedicated interactive poster hall UX. Sessionboard fits when posters are one format in a broader scientific program — abstract committee, speaker portal, platform sessions, Attendee Hub, and content reuse on one graph.

Does Sessionboard support ePosters and digital poster sessions?▼

Yes — poster submissions use the same call for papers, review workflow, speaker portal, and Attendee Hub as platform abstracts. Poster sessions appear on the published program beside platform talks.

Can we run poster tracks on Sessionboard first?▼

Yes. One annual meeting poster track is a common entry — especially when platform sessions and posters should share committee and speaker ops.

What about interactive poster boards?▼

Sessionboard prioritises program integration — acceptance, scheduling, attendee discovery, and content reuse. Highly specialised hall kiosk UX may pair with export paths during transition.

What about registration?▼

Registration can stay on your existing stack. Sessionboard owns program operations from call for papers through post-meeting content.
