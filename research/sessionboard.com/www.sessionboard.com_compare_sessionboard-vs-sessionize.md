## Quick answer

Sessionize is a speaker proposal and agenda tool for conferences and meetups — CFP intake, selection, and schedule publishing. Sessionboard adds multi-round abstract review, chair workflows, speaker portal depth, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy for associations and medical congresses that outgrow proposal-to-agenda tooling.

## Why teams choose Sessionboard

Where Sessionize stops, and where program teams keep going with Sessionboard.

## Sessionize — where it ends

- **Proposal-to-agenda speed**
Fast CFP and schedule publishing for user groups and mid-size conferences.

- **Speaker-facing UX**
Clean proposal submission and status for presenters.

- **Community and meetup scale**
Lower complexity events with simpler review needs.


### The narrow case

- Meetups and tech conferences with simple proposal selection
- Agenda publish is the finish line — no speaker CRM or post-event content

The way forward

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Enterprise abstract committee ops**
Multi-round review, chairs, rubrics, and COI on the program graph.

- **Speaker CRM and content**
Portal, transcription, Attendee Hub, and clips on session records — year over year.

- **Advocacy and registration attribution**
Tracked faculty and sponsor shares from acceptance — not agenda export alone.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Medical or scientific peer review with hundreds of submissions
- Speaker materials and program build after acceptance
- Post-meeting transcription and content reuse required
- Faculty promotion with measurable registration impact

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.
