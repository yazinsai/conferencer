## Quick answer

vFairs is an all-in-one event platform: registration, badging, apps, and venues, with an abstract module in the bundle. Sessionboard is the program-layer vFairs alternative for association and med-sci meetings: multi-round review with COI rules, speaker CRM across years, poster galleries, and post-event content. Bundle pricing picks vFairs; committee workflow picks Sessionboard.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Abstract committee chair

A form builder with review stages is not committee software — you need multi-round workflows, COI capture, chair assignments, and rubric scoring that survives 500+ submissions.

### Speaker operations manager

vFairs speaker tools live inside one event instance — you need a speaker CRM that carries bios, history, and materials across meeting years, not per-event profiles.

### Association program director

The bundle covers event weekend logistics — member value between meetings (poster galleries, session libraries, advocacy) needs a program layer that persists.

### Meeting planner evaluating suites

Bundle pricing looks efficient until the committee inherits the thin module — then review cycles run in exported spreadsheets next to the all-in-one.

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against vFairs for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

vFairs includes abstract management — why add Sessionboard?▼

Included is not the same as sufficient. The vFairs module collects submissions, assigns reviewers, and converts acceptances to sessions, which covers a light call for papers. Committee-run meetings need more: multi-round workflows with blind review and COI rules, chair tooling, rubric scoring at volume, mid-cycle form control, and a speaker record that persists across years. Teams with real committee volume end up running review in spreadsheets beside the module — which is the workflow Sessionboard replaces.

Is Sessionboard a full vFairs replacement?▼

Usually not, and that is the point. vFairs keeps registration, badging, the mobile app, and the virtual venue. Sessionboard owns the program layer: call for papers through committee review, speaker operations, and the content the meeting produces. The two run side by side, and many associations enter Sessionboard with one meeting or specialty track.

What does the all-in-one bundle actually save?▼

One contract and one project manager, which is real value for logistics. The cost shows up in the layers where depth matters: committee review, speaker history, and year-round content are where bundled modules run thinnest, and they are the layers your program committee and members touch most.

Does Sessionboard include transcription and attendee content delivery?▼

Yes — Live Transcribe capture, batch transcription, Media Library, Attendee Hub, and poster galleries are native and sit on the session record, so a speaker swap or embargo change updates every surface at once.

How does pricing compare to vFairs?▼

Both are quote-based. vFairs prices by event format, features, and volume across the whole bundle. Sessionboard prices on meeting and abstract volume for the program layer only, so you are not buying a venue platform to get committee software. Request a demo for a scoped quote.

Can we keep our AMS and registration integrations?▼

Yes. Registration stays on vFairs, Cvent, RainFocus, or your AMS. Sessionboard syncs the program layer beside them — one faculty record, no double entry.
