## Sessionboard — built in

- **Program graph from CFP to post-event**
Committee ops, speaker portal, agenda, live transcription, and Attendee Hub on one record.

- **Advocacy Agent**
Personalizes and auto-nudges advocates at acceptance, agenda, early-bird, and post-session — no manual follow-up chase.

- **Advocacy from acceptance**
Milestone prompts when speakers confirm — session title, bio, and headshot already on the graph.

- **Share → click → view → register**
Attribution through registration and on-demand watch when content lives in Sessionboard.

- **Content Marketing and Create**
Clips, quotes, and social assets from transcripts — not re-keyed from external advocacy exports.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Snöball.

- Program team accepts speakers and publishes agenda in Sessionboard — session metadata is authoritative.
- Optional: export or integrate advocate lists and assets to Snöball for teams running Snöball campaign UX.
- Snöball advocates share personalized links; Snöball reports share and conversion metrics in its dashboard.
- Sessionboard Advocacy path: acceptance and agenda milestones generate ready-to-share assets; Advocacy Agent auto-nudges advocates at each milestone.
- Registrations and Attendee Hub views attribute back to advocate and channel when Sessionboard owns registration surfaces.

**Integration status:** Sessionboard lists Snöball as a homepage integration partner. Native deep connector is lighter than Gleanin — many teams coexist via program exports or parallel campaigns. Sessionboard Advocacy is native for teams that want P2P on the program graph without a second subscription.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep Snöball only when

- Marketing is contracted on Snöball for this season's referral campaigns
- You want Snöball campaign UX plus Sessionboard CFP, review, and speaker operations
- Portfolio team runs Snöball benchmarks across shows while program ops standardizes on Sessionboard

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Advocacy should fire at speaker acceptance — not after registration opens
- You need share-to-watch attribution when sessions stream to Attendee Hub
- Program data should generate share assets automatically — no duplicate advocate import
- Medical or scientific societies want advocacy beside abstract committee workflow
- Another per-campaign advocacy SaaS line item does not fit the budget cycle

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside Snöball.

Does Sessionboard replace Snöball?▼

Not always. Snöball fits teams that want dedicated P2P campaign UX. Sessionboard native Advocacy fits when program data should drive advocacy and attribution without a parallel system.

Can we use both?▼

Yes — run Sessionboard for CFP, speakers, agenda, and content; run Snöball campaigns when marketing prefers that hub. Align on which system owns registration attribution.

How does Sessionboard compare to Snöball's published benchmarks?▼

Snöball publishes industry share-to-registration benchmarks on snoball.events — cite their report. Sessionboard measures advocacy on your program graph including watch paths when using Attendee Hub.

Is there a compare page Sessionboard vs Snöball?▼

No — we document coexistence here. See peer-to-peer event marketing for Sessionboard's native story.

What about speaker advocacy specifically?▼

Sessionboard generates personalized share moments from accepted sessions — title, abstract, and headshot — and the Advocacy Agent auto-nudges advocates at milestones so teams don't rely on manual follow-ups.
