## Quick answer

Attendee advocacy turns every registrant into a tracked referral channel, starting at the confirmation. Sessionboard Advocacy issues each attendee a personalized share page and attributed link your registration platform embeds in confirmation pages and emails via merge fields or the public API — then continues through agenda publish and post-event clips on one program graph.

## Who owns attendee advocacy?

Registration, email, and community teams each touch the attendee journey — attendee advocacy works when the same tracked link travels through all of it.

### Event marketing lead

Referral asks live in one campaign email that most attendees never open — the share link belongs in the confirmation page and every lifecycle comm, with attribution.

### Registration / ops

Your registration platform sends the confirmation — it should carry each attendee's personalized share link via merge field or API, not a generic event banner.

### Email / lifecycle marketing

Agenda publish, session picks, and know-before-you-go are your best-opened sends — each is a referral touchpoint when the attendee's tracked link rides along.

### Community / attendee marketing

Past attendees and members refer colleagues year after year — you need an advocate CRM that carries top referrers forward, not a list that resets every cycle.

## Evaluation checklist

- Does every attendee get a personalized share page and tracked link at registration — not a generic social button?
- Can your registration platform embed that link in the confirmation page and confirmation email via merge field or API?
- Do lifecycle comms — agenda publish, session picks, know-before-you-go — carry the same attributed link?
- Are attendee referrals attributed to the referring attendee and channel, and rolled up event over event?
- Does the advocate CRM carry top referrers forward to next year's event?
- Do attendee, speaker, exhibitor, and sponsor advocacy report on one graph — or in separate tools?
- Can post-event clips and session content re-activate attendees as advocates for the next edition?
- Do milestone prompts fire automatically, or does someone chase attendees by email?

## Frequently asked questions

What is attendee advocacy?▼

Equipping registered attendees with personalized share assets and tracked links so they refer colleagues to your event — designed as a journey from the registration confirmation through post-event content, with referrals attributed per attendee.

Why start at the registration confirmation?▼

It is the highest-attention moment in the attendee lifecycle — the registrant just committed and is most likely to tell colleagues. A personalized share link on the confirmation page and in the confirmation email captures that moment; a campaign email weeks later does not.

How does the share link get into our confirmation page or email?▼

Sessionboard Advocacy issues each attendee's share page and tracked link at registration. Your registration platform embeds it in confirmation pages and emails through merge fields, or your team pulls it through the Sessionboard public API — no rip-and-replace of your registration stack.

Which comms should carry the attendee's link after confirmation?▼

Every well-opened lifecycle send — agenda announcement, session-pick reminders, know-before-you-go, and post-event clip or on-demand emails. The same attributed link travels through all of them, so referrals compound instead of resetting per campaign.

How is this different from the social buttons in our registration tool?▼

Built-in buttons share a generic event page with no attribution. Attendee advocacy issues a personalized page per attendee and attributes each registration back to the referring attendee and channel — so you know who your top referrers are and can re-activate them next year from the advocate CRM.

Does attendee advocacy replace speaker advocacy?▼

No — it compounds with it. Speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees each get personalized assets at their own milestones on the same program graph, and marketing reads one attribution rollup instead of four tools.

Can we measure whether attendee referrals actually drive registrations?▼

Yes — share, click, and attributed registration per attendee and channel, rolled up event over event. That rollup is the difference between a referral program you can defend in a budget review and a share button you hope works.
