## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep EventMobi mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## EventMobi — their lane

- **Mobile event apps**
Branded apps and attendee engagement via EventMobi / Core-apps.

- **App customization**
EventMobi owns mobile UX, push, and deployment.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Program graph**
Sessions, speakers, rooms, times — authoritative before app build.

- **Review and speaker CRM**
CFP through portal on one platform; faculty persists year over year.

- **Content and Advocacy**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, clips, and tracked shares beside the app.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from EventMobi.

- Publish program in Sessionboard after review and speaker readiness.
- Export or sync agenda data into EventMobi configuration.
- Manage changes in Sessionboard — republish to EventMobi feeds.
- Link to Sessionboard Attendee Hub for on-demand content where needed.

**Integration status:** EventMobi owns mobile delivery; Sessionboard supplies program data via export or integration.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep EventMobi only when

- EventMobi app contracted for enterprise or agency-produced events
- Program complexity exceeds CSV-to-app workflow tolerance

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Small single-track event with no formal review
- No requirement for post-event content library
