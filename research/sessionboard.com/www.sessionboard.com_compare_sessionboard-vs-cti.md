## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against CTI Meeting Technology for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

What is abstract submission software?▼

Tools for call-for-papers intake, peer review, and program committee ops — on Sessionboard, extended through speaker portal, transcription, and attendee delivery on one record.

What about CTI ePoster Upload?▼

CTI offers a dedicated ePoster Upload module per congress. Sessionboard runs poster tracks on the same program graph as platform abstracts — review, speaker portal, Attendee Hub, and content reuse without a separate poster silo.

Is CTI Meeting Technology going out of business?▼

No. This page compares workflow and product fit — not vendor stability.

Can we migrate off abstractsonline.com without touching registration?▼

Yes. Most migrations move abstract intake, review, and speaker workflow first — registration stays put.

Do you support medical society conflict of interest (COI) and multi-round review?▼

Yes — COI capture, multi-round review, chair assignments, and rubrics are core. So are transcription, clips, and Attendee Hub after acceptance.

How is Sessionboard different from Cadmium if we already left CTI?▼

Cadmium is a suite play. Sessionboard focuses on abstract ops, speaker comms, and the content stack beside registration you already run.

What is the migration path from an older abstract portal?▼

Pilot one meeting or track, import historical speaker data where needed, and run parallel during an active call for papers (CFP) when possible.
