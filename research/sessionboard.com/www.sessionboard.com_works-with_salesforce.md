## Quick answer

Sessionboard works with Salesforce for event and association teams that standardize on CRM for marketing and membership. Sessionboard owns the program layer — abstracts, speakers, sessions, transcription, and advocacy — while speaker and session data can export to Salesforce Contacts, Accounts, and Campaigns. Fonteva and Nimble AMS (Salesforce-native) have dedicated works-with pages for deeper member sync.

## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep Salesforce mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## Salesforce — their lane

- **CRM system of record**
Contacts, accounts, opportunities, campaigns, and sales workflows on Salesforce.

- **Marketing and membership ops**
Teams that route event leads and member records through Salesforce objects.

- **Salesforce-native AMS**
Fonteva and Nimble AMS run on Salesforce — see dedicated Sessionboard + Fonteva / Nimble pages.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Event program graph**
CFP, review, speaker portal, agenda, and session content — purpose-built for conferences.

- **Speaker CRM across events**
Faculty identity persists across annual meetings; export enriches Salesforce contacts.

- **Content Marketing and advocacy**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Amplify — program-native, not CRM modules.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from Salesforce.

- Program team runs CFP and speakers in Sessionboard — authoritative session and speaker records.
- Speaker and session exports or API sync update Salesforce Contacts and related Campaign members.
- Registration may stay on Cvent, RainFocus, or another platform — Sessionboard links program to CRM reporting.
- Advocacy attribution events inform marketing which sessions drove pipeline influence.

**Integration status:** Salesforce export and CRM sync patterns vary by account — confirm field mapping with Sessionboard sales. For Salesforce-native AMS, prefer Sessionboard + Fonteva or Sessionboard + Nimble AMS works-with guides.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep Salesforce only when

- Enterprise CRM mandate on Salesforce for three-plus years
- Marketing ops already campaigns from Salesforce lists
- Fonteva or Nimble AMS on Salesforce for membership

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Event program still managed in spreadsheets beside Salesforce
- Need abstract review and speaker materials workflow CRM was not built for
- Content reuse and advocacy attribution should tie to session records

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside Salesforce.

Does Sessionboard replace Salesforce?▼

No — Salesforce remains CRM. Sessionboard replaces spreadsheet program ops and adds event-native depth Salesforce modules do not cover.

What about Fonteva or Nimble?▼

Those are Salesforce-native AMS platforms with dedicated Sessionboard works-with pages and deeper member sync patterns.

Can we sync speakers to Contacts?▼

Yes — export and API paths map speaker CRM to Salesforce Contacts; confirm scope with sales engineering.

Does Sessionboard include registration?▼

Registration often stays on Cvent, Swapcard, or another vendor — Sessionboard owns program and content.
