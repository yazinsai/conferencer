## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep EVA Event Tech Hub mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## EVA Event Tech Hub — their lane

- **Registration and event ops**
Attendee registration and operational workflows on EVA Event Tech Hub.

- **Event technology delivery**
Teams standardized on EVA for reg and show-floor adjacent tooling.

- **Registration system of record**
Tickets and attendee records remain on EVA for contracted events.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Abstract and reviewer operations**
Multi-round review, COI, and chair self-service on the program graph.

- **Speaker CRM through show day**
Materials, bios, and agenda truth — not re-keyed from registration exports.

- **Content Marketing stack**
Transcription, Attendee Hub, Create, and Amplify on session records.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from EVA Event Tech Hub.

- Attendees register on EVA — registration record stays the system of record.
- Accepted speakers and sessions live in Sessionboard — program team works without CSV re-keying.
- Agenda and speaker data align via export or integration — confirm with sales engineering.
- Live transcription and Attendee Hub deliver content from Sessionboard session records.

**Integration status:** EVA ↔ Sessionboard — program import/export paths available; confirm connector scope for your account with Sessionboard sales.
