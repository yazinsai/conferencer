# Built for Professionals Who Shape Event Content

This isn’t a general events workshop. It’s for the professionals responsible for content strategy, programming, and speaker ecosystems.

Association Conference Teams

Directors of Education, Conference Programming Managers, and Learning teams at trade associations and professional societies who design member-driven education experiences.

Corporate Event Teams

Event marketing executives at companies running user summits, customer conferences, and partner events with complex multi-track programming.
