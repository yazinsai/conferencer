## Quick answer

Sessionboard competes with Map Your Show on the conference and program layer only — call for papers, multi-round review, poster tracks, speaker portal, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy on a session-forward graph. Map Your Show floor plans, exhibitor sales, and attendee discovery stay in place; Sessionboard is where committee and speaker depth live when a floor-first bundle is not enough.

### VP Marketing

## Sessionboard — program, CRM & content

- **Program-forward data model**
Abstracts, posters, reviewers, and speakers — not exhibitor-record backward.

- **Med/sci and ISO committee depth**
Multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) capture, and poster tracks at congress scale.

- **Digital posters on one graph**
Poster submissions share CFP, review, speaker portal, and Attendee Hub with platform sessions — not a bolt-on author list.

- **Content marketing built in**
Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, Create, Advocacy, and Dispatch on the session record.


### When Sessionboard wins

- Informa-scale or ISO portfolio where program teams describe speaker chase unprompted
- 500+ abstracts or poster submissions with real committee volume and COI rules
- Need poster tracks, speaker portal, transcription, or Attendee Hub after acceptance
- Organiser already syncs Sessionboard to MYS — compare program depth before duplicating session tooling in the floor bundle

## What Sessionboard owns

Program, speaker CRM, content — plus Advocacy and Dispatch marketing tools competitors treat as afterthoughts.

### Program layer

Call for papers (CFP), multi-round review, chair workflows, conflict of interest (COI) tracking, and published agenda — work competitors leave in spreadsheets.

### Speaker CRM

One faculty record: portal, bio, materials, and communications across years — not a fresh CSV every submission cycle.

### Content marketing

Live transcription, Media Library, clips, Attendee Hub, and Create on the session record you already manage.

### Advocacy

Peer shares from speakers, sponsors, and faculty — tracked links to registration when sessions are accepted or confirmed.

### Dispatch

Internal team distribution — automated share recipes for staff when content is ready, beside Hootsuite or Sprout for always-on social.

## Frequently asked questions

Evaluating Sessionboard against Map Your Show session management for the program layer — not a forced rip-and-replace.

Do we replace Map Your Show entirely?▼

No. This comparison is the conference and program module only. Floor plans, exhibitor contracts, and lead retrieval stay on Map Your Show for most trade-show customers.

Is there a Sessionboard integration with Map Your Show?▼

Yes — session and speaker field mappings exist in Sessionboard for MYS sync. Program teams often run depth in Sessionboard and publish agenda context to MYS for attendee-facing discovery. Ask your rep for current sync scope on your show code.

Why compare if Map Your Show resells Sessionboard connectivity?▼

Some organisers arrive with session management bundled into the floor platform first. Compare whether committee depth, poster lifecycle, speaker CRM, and post-acceptance content are included — or whether you need program-native tooling beyond proposal intake and scheduling.

Does Sessionboard support posters and ePosters?▼

Yes — poster tracks run through the same call for papers, review, speaker portal, and Attendee Hub as platform sessions. See digital posters solution and ePoster vendor comparisons.

What about registration?▼

Registration may stay on Cvent, RainFocus, or your AMS beside MYS. Sessionboard owns program operations between acceptance and content reuse.

How does advocacy compare?▼

Sessionboard Advocacy assigns tracked peer shares from speakers and sponsors with registration attribution — fired from program events, not generic agenda share buttons.
