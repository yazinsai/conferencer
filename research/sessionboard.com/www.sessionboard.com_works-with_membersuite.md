## Quick answer

MemberSuite holds the member record, dues, and chapters. Sessionboard is the program layer: call for papers with membership validation, multi-round review, speaker portal, transcription, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy — with SSO and member sync from MemberSuite; we never create authoritative member records.

## MemberSuite — their lane

- **Authoritative member record**
Membership, dues, chapters, and contact master data in MemberSuite.

- **Member portal and SSO identity**
MemberSuite portal SSO.

- **AMS-native events and registration**
Basic event registration where contracted on the AMS stack — not deep CFP or speaker ops.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Member validation at submission**
Member record from MemberSuite; Sessionboard never replaces AMS authority.

- **Abstract and speaker program**
Multi-round review, chair workflows, speaker portal, and agenda on the program graph.

- **Content Marketing**
Transcription, Media Library, Attendee Hub, Create, Advocacy, and Dispatch on session records.

- **Content to LMS**
Session and webinar artifacts export to Path, TopClass, CloudCME, and other learning platforms — see LMS works-with pages.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from MemberSuite.

- Member or submitter authenticates via MemberSuite SSO — membership attributes available for validation.
- Sessionboard validates eligibility at call-for-papers and speaker portal — no duplicate member of record.
- Abstract committee and speaker ops run on Sessionboard through acceptance and show day.
- Transcripts, clips, and Attendee Hub content publish — optional sync to your LMS for year-round learning.
- Program participation and engagement can flow back toward MemberSuite CRM fields where mapped.

**API and import paths:** Bi-directional open API — member sync for program layer beside MemberSuite events.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep MemberSuite only when

- Multi-year MemberSuite contract with growing annual meeting program
- Membership team requires single sign-on for reviewers and submitters
- AMS event module is live but committee still runs CFP in email

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- 500+ abstracts or posters with real committee volume
- Speaker portal and content marketing required after acceptance
- Need Attendee Hub, Advocacy, or LMS content sync from Sessionboard Marketing
- Member validation at submission is non-negotiable

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside MemberSuite.

Does Sessionboard replace MemberSuite?▼

No. MemberSuite stays the membership system of record. Sessionboard owns call for papers, abstract review, speaker operations, and content marketing beside your AMS.

How does SSO work with MemberSuite?▼

MemberSuite portal SSO. Sessionboard validates submitters and reviewers against membership — ask your rep for current SSO scope.

Do you sync members from MemberSuite?▼

Yes — member identity and eligibility flow from MemberSuite for CFP and speaker portal. Sessionboard does not create authoritative member records; the AMS stays source of truth.

Can content flow to our LMS?▼

Yes — Sessionboard Content Marketing packages transcripts, clips, and Attendee Hub objects for Path, TopClass, CloudCME, and other LMS platforms. See works-with LMS pages.

What about registration on Cvent or another platform?▼

Registration can stay on Cvent, RainFocus, or EventsAir. Sessionboard connects membership from MemberSuite to program operations — registration sync is separate.

Is there a native connector?▼

Sessionboard supports SSO, member validation, and sync paths for MemberSuite. Ask your rep for connector scope on your org — deployment varies by AMS edition.
