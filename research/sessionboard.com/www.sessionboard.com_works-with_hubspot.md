## What Sessionboard delivers natively

Keep HubSpot mid-contract when you need to — Sessionboard owns call for papers, speaker operations, and content marketing on the program graph.

## HubSpot — their lane

- **CRM and marketing automation**
Contacts, companies, deals, workflows, and email nurture on HubSpot.

- **Sales pipeline and ownership**
Round-robin, lead scoring, and rep workflows marketing already standardized on.

- **Marketing website and forms**
Webflow and HubSpot-hosted forms as intake surfaces — Sessionboard enriches hidden fields.


## Sessionboard — built in

- **Program graph**
Abstracts, review, speaker portal, agenda, and session content — not native HubSpot objects.

- **Growth attribution on intake**
UTM, growth\_page\_slug, journey, entry source, and bot-risk fields on demo submissions.

- **Advocacy and registration attribution**
Share → click → registration chain from program events — feeds influenced pipeline reporting.


## How the data flows

Program and session data live in Sessionboard — with optional sync to and from HubSpot.

- Prospect visits a Sessionboard growth page or Webflow site with attribution script loaded.
- Demo or contact submit creates or updates a HubSpot contact with hidden growth fields populated.
- Inbound lead review workflow notifies Slack; approve assigns owner in HubSpot.
- Program team runs CFP and speakers in Sessionboard — speaker CRM complements HubSpot contact records.
- Marketing reports influenced pipeline using UTM and growth fields alongside HubSpot deal stages.

**Integration status:** HubSpot Private App integration for contact properties, workflows, and lead review — configured per portal. Growth attribution bridge script on Webflow and growth pages. Not a replacement for HubSpot CRM.

## Native first — add their tool only if you must

Sessionboard already includes transcription, attendee experiences, and content marketing. A separate subscription makes sense only in narrow cases.

#### Keep HubSpot only when

- Marketing and sales standardized on HubSpot for CRM and nurture
- Event program depth exceeds HubSpot custom objects or spreadsheet ops
- Demo requests need growth-page and campaign attribution in HubSpot

#### Sessionboard alone — usually the answer

- Abstract committees and speaker materials live outside HubSpot today
- Advocacy shares should attribute to registration and sync to CRM reporting
- Inbound demo review needs Slack + HubSpot approval with audit trail

## Frequently asked questions

How Sessionboard works beside HubSpot.

Does Sessionboard replace HubSpot?▼

No — Sessionboard complements HubSpot. Program operations and event content live in Sessionboard; contacts, deals, and nurture stay in HubSpot with enriched attribution on intake.

What HubSpot fields does Sessionboard populate?▼

Growth attribution set — UTMs, growth\_page\_slug, journey fields, inbound lead review status, and related properties. See growth-org/docs/HUBSPOT\_HIDDEN\_FIELDS\_CHECKLIST.md.

Does this work with Webflow forms?▼

Yes — the Webflow growth attribution script passes hidden fields into HubSpot embed forms on sessionboard.com.

Can we use HubSpot for abstract review?▼

HubSpot is not built for multi-round abstract committees. Sessionboard owns CFP and review; HubSpot receives marketing and sales handoffs.

What about Salesforce instead of HubSpot?▼

See Sessionboard + Salesforce for CRM export patterns; many teams run HubSpot for marketing and Salesforce for enterprise CRM via separate integrations.
