## Quick answer

RainFocus is the registration backbone for Adobe Summit-scale user conferences and ISO portfolios. Sessionboard sits beside RainFocus as the program layer: optional call for papers and multi-round abstract evaluation, speaker portal and faculty CRM, live transcription and Attendee Hub content marketing, and Advocacy plus Dispatch for speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees — with accepted sessions, speakers, and schedule fields syncing back to RainFocus so registration stays authoritative. Registration does not rip-and-replace; program depth compounds on one graph.

## Sound like your team?

Match your role to the job Sessionboard solves — program ops, content reuse, or marketing attribution.

### Program director

Abstract review still lives in email while registration is on RainFocus — you need CFP and committee rounds on a program graph that syncs accepted sessions back to RF.

### Speaker operations

Speaker bios and AV chase happen in spreadsheets after RF closes registration — you need one portal from acceptance through show day, with status visible to both systems.

### Marketing director

Content and advocacy are disconnected from session inventory — you need transcripts, clips, Attendee Hub, and tracked advocate shares fired from acceptance, not a generic please-share blast.

### Registration owner

RainFocus stays the system of record for attendees — program teams should push accepted speakers and sessions into RF without duplicate data entry or rip-and-replace.

## Compose RainFocus with a program layer

Registration stays on RainFocus. The decision is where call for papers, committee review, content marketing, and advocate programs run — and whether accepted speakers and sessions write back to RF without duplicate entry.

| Vendor | Best when | Tradeoffs | Pricing note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RainFocus only | Registration, attendee profile, and check-in are the scope — no formal CFP, med/sci review, or year-round content marketing on the event. | Program committees, speaker materials, and advocate workflows typically run outside RF in email and spreadsheets. | Enterprise registration suite pricing. |
| RainFocus + email / spreadsheets | Short-term workaround while RF contract is locked — reviewers and speakers coordinated manually beside registration. | No single program record; advocate and content pipelines reset every cycle; RF never receives clean session graph updates. | Hidden cost in staff time and audit risk. |
| RainFocus + Sessionboard | Optional CFP and multi-round evaluation in Sessionboard; speaker/exhibitor/sponsor/attendee advocacy and content marketing on the program graph; accepted sessions and speakers write back to RainFocus for registration-bound workflows. | Requires adopting a program layer beside RF — confirm current sync and write-back patterns on your demo. | Program layer scoped to abstract, speaker, and content volume. |
| Rip-and-replace RainFocus | Rare — multi-year RF contract and registration mandate make this impractical for most enterprises. | High switching cost; unnecessary when Sessionboard layers beside registration. | N/A for most ISO-scale buyers. |

## Evaluation checklist

- Can call for papers and multi-round abstract review run in Sessionboard while RainFocus stays registration system of record?
- Do accepted sessions, speakers, and schedule fields write back to RainFocus without duplicate entry?
- Does Advocacy cover speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees with tracked links from program state?
- Is content marketing — transcription, clips, Attendee Hub, Create — on the same graph as CFP and speaker portal?
- Does Dispatch reach internal teams (membership, sales, GTM) from the same session inventory?
- Is conflict-of-interest capture required for med/sci review — and handled in the program layer?
- Do program teams need SME and faculty history across multiple RainFocus events in the portfolio?

## Frequently asked questions

Can we keep RainFocus for registration?▼

Yes — always. Sessionboard targets program, speaker, content, and advocate workflows beside RainFocus. Registration, badges, and attendee profile stay on RF.

Can call for papers run in Sessionboard and sync to RainFocus?▼

Yes — that is the recommended pattern for teams that need multi-round review, COI capture, or med/sci committee ops RF does not own. Accepted abstracts become sessions and speakers on the Sessionboard graph; implementers sync accepted roster and schedule fields back to RainFocus — confirm connector and field mapping on your demo call.

What does Sessionboard add beside RainFocus?▼

Optional CFP and evaluation, speaker portal and faculty CRM, published agenda and content capture, Attendee Hub, and Advocacy plus Dispatch for speakers, exhibitors, sponsors, and attendees — all on one program record that can write back to RF.

Does Sessionboard integrate officially with RainFocus?▼

RainFocus does not publish an open program-layer partnership; Sessionboard operates beside RF via export, API, and sync patterns buyers and implementers configure. Confirm current write-back status for your stack on a demo — do not assume a single turnkey SKU.

What about RainFocus Event MCP?▼

Registration MCP covers attendee and check-in data for AI assistants. Sessionboard Event MCP exposes the speaker, session, abstract, and content-readiness graph program chairs change daily — composable stack, not one dataset.

Compare pages for RainFocus?▼

We publish coexistence guides, not suite compare attack pages. The buyer question is program depth beside registration — not rip-and-replace.
