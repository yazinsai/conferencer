## Why poster programs fragment

Poster authors treated as a separate population — disconnected from faculty CRM, agenda, and post-meeting content reuse.

- Poster submission tools stop at review — author identity does not flow to registration badges or faculty history.
- Interactive ePoster UX siloed from platform session capture — marketing cannot reuse poster content post-meeting.
- Poster tours scheduled manually while platform agenda lives in Sessionboard or another ops tool.
- Attendees hunt QR codes and PDF lists instead of searchable poster discovery in the attendee program.

## How posters fit the program graph

One speaker identity and session record across platform talks, posters, and on-demand publish.

- Call for papers includes poster tracks → same review rounds, chair workflows, and acceptance state as platform abstracts.
- Authors confirmed → speaker portal collects poster files, bios, and presentation preferences — one checklist per presenter.
- Poster sessions scheduled → tours, boards, and rooms appear on the published program beside platform talks.
- Attendee discovery → the Poster Gallery publishes a branded, linkable hall with instant search, bookmarks, and author details tied to session records.
- Optional live capture → author presentations transcribed into Media Library when poster sessions include synchronous Q&A.

## Frequently asked questions

Poster sessions as part of the congress — not an isolated submission tool.

Do you replace dedicated ePoster vendors?▼

Sessionboard fits when posters are one format in a broader program — abstract review, speaker portal, agenda, and the published Poster Gallery matter alongside poster UX. Poster-only buys may still use specialized boards; see compare pages for honest fit criteria.

Can we run an online poster hall?▼

Yes. The Poster Gallery publishes a branded, linkable hall with instant search, in-browser viewing of PDF, video, and image posters, bookmarks, embargo timing, and per-poster engagement analytics. See the Poster Gallery solution page for the full walkthrough.

How do poster authors relate to platform speakers?▼

One speaker identity across formats — faculty who present posters and talks do not maintain separate profiles each cycle.

Does this work for virtual poster halls?▼

Yes. The Poster Gallery serves remote discovery and bookmarks from one link. Hybrid meetings use the same graph for onsite tours and online poster browsing.

What about poster numbering and floor plans?▼

Poster sessions participate in program build — numbering and schedule blocks align with ops workflows for large halls.

Can we compare Sessionboard to Learning Toolbox or iPosterSessions?▼

Yes — see vendor comparison pages for ePoster-first tools versus program-native posture.

When is a poster-only tool enough?▼

Small poster-only virtual fairs without formal review or platform sessions may not need a full program layer. Add Sessionboard when poster authors share a committee with platform faculty.
